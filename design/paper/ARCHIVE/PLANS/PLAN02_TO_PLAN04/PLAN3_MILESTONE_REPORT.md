# Plan-3 Milestone Report

## Scientific shift

Plan 3 moved beyond a scalar PC claim. The evidence now supports an error-structure account: examination and sampling geometry dominate architecture choice; most squared error is shared across reconstruction contexts; physical disagreement exposes the smaller context-sensitive component; and shared anchors create a predictable stable-wrong regime when the target follows a nonlinear through-plane trajectory.

## Strong results

1. **Latent difficulty:** case effects explain 58.3% fastMRI and 83.9% NingXia variance. Eleven neural model families share median error rho 0.961; six IXI methods share 0.991.
2. **Universal sampled-factor ordering in scope:** all 138 case–architecture units satisfy x2 < x4 < x6 error.
3. **Exact decomposition:** shared consensus error is 80.9%/86.5% of three-context MSE; observable diversity is 19.1%/13.5%.
4. **Boundary mechanism:** NingXia x4–x6 first-plane stable-wrong enrichment survives case clustering. Shared-anchor outputs show lower disagreement despite higher error; target curvature is elevated; synthetic anchor deletion changes only the first interval.
5. **Generic perturbation comparison:** PC improves nAURC over flip-TTA in all 18 cohort–factor–architecture profiles; clustered p=0.0035 fastMRI and 0.0015 NingXia.
6. **Pre-reconstruction signal:** adjacent acquired-slice RMSE predicts error in fastMRI, NingXia, and 115-case IXI, but scale-controlled strength is domain dependent.

## Important falsifications

- More error diversity alone does not predict a better witness.
- High-frequency disagreement is not the primary PC mechanism.
- Disagreement-triangle outliers do not attribute the failing factor; constant x6 is perfect in the observed panel.
- Motion magnitude is not a cross-cohort dominant explanation.
- Naive PC/input-score combinations harm ranking and do not solve the blind spot universally.

## Interpretation

The general design principle must be more careful than “maximize diversity.” A useful intervention must preserve the target, avoid merely changing scale, and expose error components not already shared by contexts. Current physical factors succeed at witnessing a minority context-sensitive component but cannot observe the dominant consensus component. Input anchor change is a mechanistically complementary proxy, not a certificate.
