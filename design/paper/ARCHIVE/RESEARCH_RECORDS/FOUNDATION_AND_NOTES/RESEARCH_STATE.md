## New comparative result

Physical sampling context beats four-view flip-TTA in all 18 combinations of two cohorts, three factors, and three architectures. Mean delta nAURC is -0.0340 (`p=0.0035`) fastMRI and -0.1143 (`p=0.0015`) NingXia using examination-clustered swaps. This does not imply superiority over arbitrary perturbations.
