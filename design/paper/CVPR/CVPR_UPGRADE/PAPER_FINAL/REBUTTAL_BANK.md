# CVPR rebuttal bank

## 1. “There is no capability beyond adjacent difference or output PC.”

The experiments support this criticism for the tested method branches. Minimum-influence selection, multi-anchor jackknife and covariance-derived residualization all failed locked confirmation/transfer gates and were not retuned. The paper is positioned as a mechanism/findings contribution, not a superior-score paper. If a new method is mandatory for the reviewer’s venue interpretation, this limitation cannot be rebutted rhetorically.

## 2. “Finite-difference influence is a perturbation artifact.”

P7-CV-E02 varies amplitude, sign, Rademacher/Gaussian field, direction count and physical plane for HAT/SwinIR on all three cohorts under a per-condition rank-stability gate. All 42 amplitude/sign/field/direction strata pass with rank stability .942–.999. Plane pairs are less stable (.779–.954): only one of three plane-pair gates passes. We therefore define the statistic as a stable local probe at a declared plane, not a plane-invariant attribution. This narrows rather than conceals the result.

## 3. “Overlap count and geometry explain everything.”

In four contexts, overlap count is exactly one while target, model, preprocessing and input count are fixed; span and asymmetry are controlled. Continuous influence supports alignment/shared fraction on fastMRI and IXI but not universally on NingXia. For two bracketing anchors, changing identity overlap while holding the entire signed distance set fixed is combinatorially impossible, and this feasibility boundary is disclosed.

## 4. “You call a correlational chain causal.”

We do not. Each arrow is a conditional association with examination/patient-cluster intervals. The final context-ranking arrow uses only eight configurations and is heterogeneous. Neither causal mediation nor intervention on a well-defined treatment is claimed.

## 5. “This duplicates the TMI paper.”

TMI owns the comprehensive physical-overlap characterization, endpoints/factors and operational MRI QA. CVPR owns nonlinear shared-influence measurement, fixed-count tests, arrow-wise mechanism and failed capability gates. After the Plan7 rewrite, no audited TMI words fall in paragraph matches above the 0.45 diagnostic threshold, but scientific overlap remains moderate-to-high; therefore the internal recommendation is `MERGE_OR_SEQUENCE_REQUIRED`, not concurrent submission without policy review and disclosure.
