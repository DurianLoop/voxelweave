# Verified CVPR format and policy snapshot

Verified 2026-08-13 from the official CVPR 2026 author guidelines and `cvpr-org/author-kit`. No CVPR 2027 kit was available; replace this snapshot when the actual target-year rules are published.

- Official review style: 10 pt, two-column, US letter, `cvpr.sty` review mode.
- Main paper: maximum eight pages including figures and tables; additional reference-only pages allowed.
- Double blind: omit authors, affiliations, acknowledgments, and identifying links.
- Submission system: OpenReview; complete author profiles required.
- Supplement is optional and reviewers are not obligated to read it.
- Human-subject/personal data require careful ethical handling and disclosure.
- Dual submission: CVPR 2026 prohibits substantially similar concurrent work and defines 20% or more overlap as substantially similar for the stated rule. The local TMI draft therefore creates `SUBMISSION_CONFLICT_RISK` and must not be concurrently submitted without a venue-policy decision.

Official sources:

- https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuidelines
- https://github.com/cvpr-org/author-kit
