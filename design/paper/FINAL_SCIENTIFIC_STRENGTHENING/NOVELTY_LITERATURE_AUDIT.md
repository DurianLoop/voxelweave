## Intended novelty statement

Common or correlated predictor error is not itself new. The contribution is a controlled intervention on the identity of shared physical evidence, a local sensitivity account of how that evidence enters error covariance, and a falsifiable audit of which target-error components remain observable in disagreement.

This statement does not establish exclusive priority over every possible input-dependence study. The covariance proposition uses standard local-linear and second-moment mathematics. Its role is to state assumptions and connect the implemented probe to the experiment, not to claim a new universal ensemble theorem. New results must determine whether the physical-evidence explanation survives cross-architecture pairs and strict acquired-only preprocessing. Regardless of outcome, score superiority remains unestablished.
