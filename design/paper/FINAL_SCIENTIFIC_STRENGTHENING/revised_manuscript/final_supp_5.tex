\section{Negative Results and Boundaries}
\subsection{Final-analysis boundaries}
Every fastMRI fixed-reference ranking interval crosses zero; SAINT-to-I3Net has a negative point estimate. Four IXI fixed-reference intervals cross zero, all with I3Net as companion. The more favorable condition-specific paired-error endpoint is separately labeled and cannot replace fixed-reference inference. Near-orthogonal but no opposing neural sensitivities occur in the fixed-context sample, and individual approximation errors can exceed 100\%. Acquired-only brain influence does not repair the old NingXia null/mixed result or establish a cross-architecture finite-response claim. The final score-search branch is closed.

\subsection{One Acquired-Only Guard: Full Negative Evaluation}
This experiment uses stored phase-0 predictions from two fixed pairs, HAT/SwinIR and I3Net/CycleINR. Each pair is evaluated on five target planes per examination. A target's scoring mask is the union of its two closest acquired phase-0 1\% foregrounds. A single acquired nonzero-intensity Q99--Q1 range normalizes disagreement, error and acquired-anchor baselines. Both the mask and scale are computed without the missing target. Error evaluation then uses that same support. Model weights are dataset-specific historical checkpoints, not one cross-dataset model.

The 24 fastMRI case IDs are ordered by SHA-256 of a fixed prefix plus ID; the first 12 form development and the others internal evaluation. All 115 IXI cases are an additional historically reused transfer cohort. No patient appears in both new fastMRI parts, but historical analysis reuse prevents an untouched-holdout claim.

Only one candidate is tried: $G=\max(F_{\rm dev}(D_n),F_{\rm dev}(A_n))$, with right-continuous development empirical CDFs and acquired-range normalization. The dependency trigger is shared phase-0 evidence; no claim is made that the guard estimates neural influence. The thresholds are development Q75 of $\min(\mathrm{MAE}_a,\mathrm{MAE}_b)_n$ and Q25 of $D_n$. This requires both predictions to have high case-level error and low disagreement. Formula, CDF samples and thresholds are saved before the evaluation summary is computed. There is no threshold retuning or additional formula search.

\subsection{Material Negative and Boundary Results}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Integer overlap is not a monotone dose; the exact analytical ladder reverses at zero-to-one sharing.
\item Fixed-count continuous influence is null/mixed on NingXia; all eight-patient intervals cross zero.
\item Local response rankings are stable to tested perturbation parameters but not invariant across physical planes.
\item The local cancellation chain is stronger than the global context-ranking chain; no causal mediation claim is supported.
\item Anchor-CV is not uniquely informative or universally superior. Adjacent differences are statistically similar and can be stronger; output PC wins several IXI comparisons.
\item Examination scores do not localize the worst plane and are not calibrated pixelwise uncertainty.
\item Densification results are retrospective; the same-model audit retains a negative NingXia Res-SRDiff boundary and omits scanner-time, motion, and clinical utility.
\item Minimum-influence context selection transfers below random/PC on IXI and is heterogeneous on NingXia.
\item Multi-anchor jackknife IQR collapses from development to confirmation/transfer and is far below adjacent MAE.
\item Covariance residualization gives negligible IXI gain with intervals crossing zero and mixed/tied results elsewhere.
\end{enumerate}

Items 8--10 test abandoned mechanism-derived methods. They are not main-paper contributions, but are retained here because they prevent the reader from inferring that the mechanism already yields an improved universal score.
