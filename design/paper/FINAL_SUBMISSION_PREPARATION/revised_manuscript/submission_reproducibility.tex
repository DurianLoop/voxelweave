\section{Reproducibility and Evidence Map}
\subsection{Executable reproduction and report cards}
Run \texttt{python reproduce\_main.py --complete} in \path{FINAL_SUBMISSION_PREPARATION/}. The CPU-only path reads preserved target, prediction and finite-response arrays; reconstructs the historical and primary observation tables and clustered statistics; independently remeasures the support and geometry sensitivities; and regenerates the main figures, tables and report cards. Historical summaries are assertion targets, not numerical inputs. Historical observation keys still define the frozen selections. No training or network inference is required. Array bindings, differences and tolerances are recorded in \path{REPRODUCTION_CHECK.md}; environment, commands and detailed legacy paths are in \path{REPRODUCIBILITY.md}.

The report card separates local observability, the named ranking endpoint, beyond-count influence, fixed-count association, stability, information access and claim eligibility. Unmeasured endpoints are NA. Cross-architecture phase rows do not inherit within-model response evidence, and probe rows do not inherit phase-ranking effects. Eligibility refers to the named claim under its contract, not individual reconstruction correctness.

\subsection{Frozen evidence and post-hoc patch}
The accepted primary studies remain under \path{FINAL_SCIENTIFIC_STRENGTHENING/}. The submission patch separately preserves the accepted manuscript and final tables and hashes all array inputs before sensitivity calculation. Its \path{FINAL_SUBMISSION_PATCH_PROTOCOL.md} distinguishes frozen primary evidence, new post-hoc sensitivities and editorial changes; \path{freeze.json} binds the protocol and manifests.

The three complete sensitivity files have stems \texttt{CROSS\_ARCH\_SUPPORT\_SENSITIVITY}, \texttt{CROSS\_ARCH\_GEOMETRY\_SENSITIVITY} and \texttt{CROSS\_ARCH\_FAMILY\_ROBUSTNESS}. They retain every ordered pair, family summary and unavailable geometry contract. The acquired-union rule is the existing foreground-union protocol; mirror comparison follows the historical reflection-class analysis while retaining the new study's mean estimand. Independent pixel/geometry reconstruction is distinguished from reuse of the frozen bootstrap aggregation code.

\subsection{Provenance, negative results and access boundary}
The companion claim matrix maps every main claim to its unit, array source, uncertainty contract and figure/table. The research ledger retains historical clean controls, analytical ladders, response/stability audits, recovered-model-bank and budget results, densification and external MRA studies, and every qualified adverse result. Detailed workspace paths and protocol amendments are retained there rather than repeated in the scientific narrative.

The local review package includes the manuscript and reachable figure/table sources; it is not a redistribution of clinical source arrays. The data/ethics/citation audit records verified attribution separately from unresolved release, authorization and de-identification documentation. Numerical reproduction cannot resolve those author responsibilities, restore an untouched cohort, or account for the full history of research selection.
