# Venue calibration: TMI and CVPR

Audit date: 2026-08-13. Policy statements below use official venue pages only. Paper calibration uses publisher/index/proceedings pages; it is a scientific comparison set, not policy.

## 1. Official policy calibration

### IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging

- The current [TMI submission checklist](https://ieeetmi.org/submission-checklist/) and [author instructions](https://ieeetmi.org/authors-instructions/) limit an initial regular-paper submission to **10 pages including references**, at 10-point type or larger. Published pages 9–10 incur mandatory overlength charges; this is a cost issue, not a scientific reason to stay at eight pages.
- TMI's [official publication criteria](https://ieeetmi.org/key-criteria-for-publication/) are novelty, technical/writing quality, scope appropriateness, and non-incremental impact. The 2025 editorial criteria explicitly include reconstruction, uncertainty, instability and hallucination within imaging-method evaluation.
- Consequence for this paper: use extra space only for the operational QA decision, inferential hierarchy and deployment assumptions. Do not claim diagnostic validation. A cover letter is prepared because Plan7 requests it, but the official instructions discourage routine cover letters unless explaining prior review or a conference extension.

### CVPR 2026

- The [official author guidelines](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuidelines) allow **eight content pages**, including figures/tables, with additional reference-only pages. Supplementary material is optional and must remain self-contained.
- The [reviewer guidelines](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines) and [training material](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/ReviewerTrainingMaterial) ask whether a paper is technically sound and makes a meaningful methodological, empirical, or conceptual contribution. Novelty and potential impact are weighed with performance; failure to exceed SOTA is not by itself a rejection reason.
- Consequence for this paper: the mechanism/findings route is legitimate only if the shared-influence measurement is stable, the count/diversity alternatives are genuinely falsified, and the negative capability results are explicit. A weak method-superiority story is worse calibrated than a precise empirical/mechanistic contribution.

### TMI set-level pattern

The strongest calibrated TMI papers combine a nontrivial imaging problem, an imaging/physics-specific method or intervention, multiple relevant validation regimes, and a practical or scientific consequence. Most do not rely on a single algebraic identity. This supports centering the submission on the controlled physical measurement intervention, three-cohort endpoint/factor characterization, selective QA consequence and explicit operating regimes. It argues against presenting consensus blindness as a new uncertainty identity.

### CVPR set-level pattern

Most accepted comparators present a method/capability with broad validation; the smaller findings class isolates a general mechanism with decisive controls and robustness. The current MRI paper does not match the former after P7-CV-E01/E03/E04 failed. Its defensible route is a scoped mechanism/findings paper: continuous estimator influence improves on integer overlap as an explanatory variable, while three failed method branches delimit what the mechanism does **not** immediately enable. This is lower novelty than the strongest CVPR method set and raises a real `MERGE_OR_SEQUENCE_REQUIRED` risk if its scientific evidence materially overlaps TMI.

## 4. Manuscript-level calibration decisions

| Decision | TMI | CVPR |
|---|---|---|
| Core contribution | Physical measurement-overlap intervention and MRI QA consequences | Nonlinear shared-influence measurement and falsification of count-only/context-selection/correction claims |
| Evidence expected in main paper | Three cohorts, endpoints, factors, risk, false retention, operating assumptions | Stability grid, fixed-count evidence, arrow-wise chain, counterexamples and failed method gates |
| Claim explicitly prohibited | New disagreement identity; clinical safety; universal best score | Anchor-CV superiority; causal mediation; universal neural influence law; new capability after failed gates |
| Recommended venue posture | TMI regular paper, ready after human metadata if final audit passes | Mechanism/findings candidate; sequence after TMI or substantially separate before submission |
