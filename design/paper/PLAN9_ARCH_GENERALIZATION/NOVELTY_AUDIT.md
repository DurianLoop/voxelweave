# Plan-9 novelty audit

The full primary-source review and nearest-concept matrix are in `LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md`.

## Decision

Plan 9 supports a scoped novelty statement: the study manipulates physical measurement dependence while holding the missing MRI target fixed, then links that intervention to error alignment and disagreement observability across several interpolation interfaces.

It does not support “first,” “architecture independent,” “influence always beats count,” or “disjoint contexts always improve ranking.” None of the audited I3Net, SAINT, or CycleINR reconstruction papers studies same-target deterministic disagreement reliability, error cancellation, or shared physical-measurement influence. Their architectural diversity strengthens the falsification design but does not make the local reproductions exact official implementations.

The most important novelty boundary is empirical: only SAINT passes all new-architecture finite-response stability gates, its fixed-count influence interval crosses zero, and the downstream ranking benefit is heterogeneous. The revised novelty is therefore the controlled physical-dependence/error-geometry finding and its openly measured architecture boundary—not universal influence superiority.
