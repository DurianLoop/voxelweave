## 2026-08-13 — Plan 4 controlled overlap intervention

- Synthesized phase-0 and phase-2 x4 acquisitions from dense evaluation volumes. They preserve target planes while sharing zero anchors; dense targets are never used in the disagreement score.
- Added a maximal-overlap control using through-plane reversal with the same two anchors and a common target-free foreground mask.
- In exact case/model/plane pairs, zero overlap improved error correlation by 0.355 on fastMRI (case-cluster 95% CI 0.225–0.557) and 0.193 on NingXia OOF (0.111–0.306).
- The effect replicated separately for HAT and SwinIR. In fastMRI, zero-overlap disagreement was smaller in raw magnitude (0.59–0.77×) yet more informative, rejecting the “larger perturbation” explanation.
- Linear and cubic interpolators independently produced case Spearman 0.958–0.977, rejecting a Transformer-only explanation.

## 2026-08-13 — Curvature, deployment, and falsification

- A development-fitted curvature–spacing model reached rho 0.722 and R² 0.479 on held-out fastMRI cases, above spacing-only 0.595/0.329.
- Absolute calibration did not transfer to NingXia. The no-fit curvature–spacing rank retained rho 0.606, but simple anchor change was better on fastMRI and worse on NingXia. A universal single-feature law was rejected.
- The disjoint witness predicted realized x4-to-x2 improvement (rho 0.811/0.626), but acquiring both x4 phases already costs x2. The prospective adaptive-acquisition claim was therefore withdrawn as circular.
- A deployable interleaved-grid variant using an existing x2 acquisition was strong but did not beat ordinary x2–x4 PC. The contribution is not promoted as a universal estimator replacement.
- Final central claim was narrowed to an experimentally validated reliability-intervention principle and controlled stress-test protocol.
