# TMI submission strategy

The compiled manuscript in `TMI_MANUSCRIPT/` is the frozen Plan-1–4 scientific record. New Plan5 IXI overlap, covariance, clean-control, bipartition, and allocation evidence is excluded; IXI appears only in the pre-Plan5 auxiliary difficulty analysis.

`SUBMISSION_CONFLICT_RISK`: the CVPR research branch shares the central consensus-error discovery and measurement-overlap premise. Do not submit the present TMI and any derivative conference manuscript concurrently without an explicit venue-policy decision, disclosure, and sufficient scientific distinction.

Before TMI upload, authors must supply the names/affiliations/ORCIDs, funding, exact NingXia ethics/consent wording, public-data ethics wording, code/data availability, and any required AI-use disclosure listed in `TMI_MANUSCRIPT/TMI_COMPLIANCE_CHECK.md`.
