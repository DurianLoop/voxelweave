# Frozen-record supersession notice

Existing Plan-1--Plan-4 artifacts remain immutable. This notice corrects two data-description and inferential issues without changing historical files.

## Anatomy

The NingXia-T2 cohort is knee MRI, not brain MRI. Repository evidence is explicit in `docx/progress/Jun/6.2.md` (NingXia-T2 is the local knee-MRI line), while IXI-T2 and fastMRI-brain-T2 are brain MRI. Historical phrases such as “two primary brain cohorts” are superseded. Numerical reconstruction results are unaffected.

## Independent unit

The 22 NingXia NIfTI series belong to eight patient groups, identified by the stable `NX001`--`NX008` prefixes and used as the outer-CV grouping in `design/rasinet/cross_scale_protocol.py`. Historical analyses that resampled 22 series as independent examinations understate dependence. The TMI manuscript therefore reports 22 series from eight patients and uses patient-prefix clustered inference generated by `scripts/tmi_statistical_corrections.py`.

The original point estimates remain descriptive. Superseding confidence intervals and permutation results are in `tables/ningxia_patient_cluster_corrections.json`. Claims are downgraded if the corrected interval/test does not support them.
