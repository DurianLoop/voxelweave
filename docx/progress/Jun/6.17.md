## HAT and SMamba/Mamba IXI-T2 Screening

Added two IXI-T2 x4 screening packages:

- `ixi_hat_2p5d`: HAT-style 2.5D residual reproduction adapted from the public HAT architecture family.
- `ixi_smamba_unet_2p5d`: SMamba-UNet/MambaIR-style 2.5D residual reproduction with a directional selective scan surrogate.

Interpretation:

1. HAT-style 2.5D beats SwinIR-style 2.5D on IXI-T2 mini and should be expanded before other new baselines.
2. SMamba-UNet/MambaIR-style is useful as a 2024+ architecture control but does not currently beat SwinIR/HAT; keep it lower priority.
