# Original-acquisition slice identity: source qualification gate

**Purpose:** A future experiment that claims original measurement-identity dependence needs a source whose individual candidate planes can be traced to acquisition events. The current [public-source manuscript](PUBLIC_ONLY_MIRA_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md) only claims dependence on **published-slice context**. Its positive results, the linear-partner negative control and the synthetic matched-geometry noise experiment do not substitute for the gate below.

## Required before target-pixel analysis

1. Establish an official release/accession and a usable research/publication license for the exact files. Record the official citation, download URL/API, immutable inventory and per-file hash. A local folder name or a similar paper's dataset agreement is insufficient.
2. At the series level, record the scanner sequence, 2D/3D acquisition mode, full DICOM `ImageType`, source/derivation fields, slice thickness, center spacing, orientation, per-plane physical position and any export/reformatting history. `ORIGINAL/PRIMARY` is useful but not by itself proof that every image is independently acquired; `MPR` requires explicit source documentation before making an original-plane claim.
3. Require a header-only rule for patient and series inclusion, physical layer ordering, target positions and minimum valid layer count. Fix all eligible IDs, model weights, scripts, endpoint, patient-level statistic, failure handling and source/weight hashes **before decoding held-out target pixels**. Keep complete failures and protocol strata.
4. To estimate a *pure* acquisition-identity effect, match physical location, neighboring anatomical content, distance/position encoding, normalization and reconstruction processing while changing only an independently acquired measurement realization. Two shifted phase grids in one volume cannot supply this counterfactual. A repeated scan at the same slice centers with documented independence, or known forward-model simulation clearly labeled as synthetic, is required. Repeated real scans also need motion/registration and noise-level controls.
5. Distinguish retrospective error observability (requires target image) from a deployable error detector, and distinguish native-plane interpolation from true thick-to-thin recovery (requires an independently acquired thin reference or a defensible forward-model simulation).

On 2026-10-01, a read-only attempt to query the official TCIA collection API failed because the configured local network proxy could not connect; no negative conclusion about other TCIA collections follows. This gate can be applied when official connectivity returns or when the author supplies verifiable acquisition and release records. No current local candidate meets every item, and that is a **data-provenance limitation**, not an experimental failure of the context audit.

**Broader local-screen update (supersedes the seven-case coverage in the table).** The [frozen all-multi-T2 screen](FASTMRI_ALL_T2_HEADER_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) checked 61 manifest cases and 2,944 nonpixel DICOM headers. Four of 14 pairs with two DICOM T2 labels matched the checked geometry and protocol, including two without `REPEAT`; none had usable distinct acquisition/series timing. The 138 manifest T2 rows correspond to only 71 T2-labeled DICOM UID series in this export, requiring a source-pipeline reconciliation. This adds trace-back leads, not eligibility for a target-pixel or causal acquisition-identity claim; source rights and independent measurement records remain absent.

**Manifest-linked clarification.** A [separately frozen crosswalk and pair screen](FASTMRI_MANIFEST_LINKED_PAIR_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found 122 unique UIDs behind the 138 manifest T2 rows, with 55 UIDs lacking literal `T2` in their DICOM description. Comparing all 64 manifest-linked UID pairs, including those with nonliteral labels, adds no technical match beyond the same four; all 64 lack usable timing. The earlier 14 literal-label pairs and these 64 pairs overlap and do not supply independent confirmations. The original-plane gate remains failed.

**External repeat-source check.** A [metadata-only screen](REPEAT_ACQUISITION_SOURCE_SCREEN_2026-10-01.md) found a five-person Zenodo T2w scan-rescan set whose accompanying EULA restricts use/copying, and TCIA RIDER Neuro MRI's 19 repeat-scan patients under NIH controlled access. Neither source was downloaded or pixel-tested; neither currently clears rights, matching-plane and header eligibility gates.

**Public QIN repeat-T2 full-cohort update.** The [preregistered TCIA header-only screen](QIN_REPEAT_T2_ALL_HEADER_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) downloaded and verified all 30 axial T2 series for 15 patients. The series document distinct original/primary 2D repeat examinations, but 0/15 pairs meet the exact-plane geometry rule: every pair differs in orientation and in the paired first-pixel position grid or layer count. An independent recomputation of true field-of-view centers also finds no match among the eight equal-count pairs. The frozen screen mislabeled DICOM `ImagePositionPatient` as a center; the correction does not change eligibility. This prostate source is not a brain external confirmation and does not isolate acquisition identity.

**UPENN-GBM official-source update.** [Frozen metadata and one-series header screening](UPENN_GBM_T2_SOURCE_AND_HEADER_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) first found a licensed axial T2-labelled stratum of 402 series/374 patients and a `DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER` pilot. A separate, explicitly **processed published-plane** protocol then selected one series per patient and independently verified [all 374 selected ZIPs and 23,702 nonpixel headers](UPENN_GBM_PROCESSED_T2_HEADER_RESULT_2026-10-01.md): **374/374** selected series are derived/secondary, despite `MRAcquisitionType=2D`. Their 3.0/4.8 mm physical steps contradict the uniform 1 mm `SpacingBetweenSlices` tag. The later [CPU pixel audit](UPENN_GBM_PROCESSED_T2_CONTEXT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) supports only released-plane context dependence. This full selected-stratum finding does not classify the other 28 candidate series or every UPENN T2 sequence, and cannot pass the original-acquisition identity gate.

**Thin-3D metadata branch:** [corrected-label official CSVs and one preselected nonpixel header pilot](UPENN_GBM_THIN_T2_HEADER_PILOT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found an approximately 0.9 mm T2 SAG SPACE released grid in the first of 83 candidate series. All 192 pilot DICOMs are DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER despite 3D acquisition-type and 0.9 mm physical step; source-image chain is absent. This supports only a processed thin-grid source lead, not original independently acquired thin-plane truth. It remains separate from the 374 processed axial T2 cases.

**TCGA-GBM metadata availability update.** Under a [frozen official-only screen](TCGA_GBM_T2_SOURCE_SCREEN_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), the TCIA page was accessible, but the NBIA MR series query returned HTTP 200 with an empty body and the official collection enumeration lacked the exact `TCGA-GBM` name. Patient/series counts and file rights are unknown; this is an access/identifier question, not a negative T2 finding. No DICOM or pixels were read, and this source cannot yet enter the original-plane gate.
