# High-resolution 3D T2 pilot: primary SwinIR replication fails

The [outcome-independent source gate](DS007045_SOURCE_QUALIFICATION_AND_ACQUISITION_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found only 34/363 current `ds007045` cases with raw T2 metadata indicating 3D acquisition and `SliceThickness<=1.5 mm`; all 34 passed processed T2/mask geometry and label-target qualification. The [frozen pilot rule](DS007045_THIN3D_MODEL_PILOT_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) was written before model inference. The [case results and summary](experiments/ds007045_thin3d_model_pilot/summary.json) attempted all 34 on CPU with the fixed IXI HAT/SwinIR checkpoints. `sub-NSK47` timed out after the protocol's one network retry and remains a failed case; 33 people/132 tumor-positive withheld targets completed. The [independent source-hash and arithmetic check](experiments/ds007045_thin3d_model_pilot/independent_full_verification.json) passed on all 33 cases, 132 targets, eight difference intervals and three site summaries.

Primary comparison: patient-equal, observed-anchor-range-normalized MSE of **SwinIR minus model-pipeline linear** using the same phase-0 observed slices, anchor normalization, 256-pixel resize, clipping and output resize. Positive means SwinIR is worse.

| ROI | Mean difference | 95% patient-bootstrap interval | Positive cases |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tumor label in acquired-anchor support | -0.00000340 | [-0.00004633, +0.00004103] | 14/33 |
| Full acquired-anchor support | -0.00078717 | [-0.00089638, -0.00067631] | 0/33 |

SwinIR improves the full-support metric, but its tumor-region difference is unresolved and slightly negative in the sample mean. The preregistered directional pilot gate **fails**. Simultaneous tumor-worse/full-better sign occurs in 14/33 (42.4%; Wilson 95% lower bound 27.2%), but that sign count does not override the null tumor mean or establish clinical harm. BA contributes 2 patients, FO 1, NSK 30, so the aggregate is dominated by one site.

The prespecified **secondary** HAT comparison has tumor minus pipeline-linear normalized MSE +0.00015243 (bootstrap interval +0.00008882 to +0.00021521; 26/33 patients positive) and full-support difference -0.00079957 (interval -0.00092218 to -0.00067835; 0/33 positive). This is an exploratory model-specific regional sign reversal, **not a replacement primary success** after the SwinIR result. It needs a separately frozen independent high-resolution T2 source, a patient-level source-overlap audit, and a direct task consequence before any broad or clinical claim.

A [post-result robustness check](experiments/ds007045_thin3d_model_pilot/hat_secondary_sensitivity.json) finds HAT tumor-worse/full-better in 26/33; its tumor difference remains positive within NSK alone (23/30 patients, mean +0.00014577, exploratory interval +0.00007712 to +0.00021488). A descriptive eight-comparison Bonferroni bootstrap interval for the full 33-patient HAT tumor difference is +0.00006755 to +0.00023789. These calculations reduce concern that the observed sign is driven only by the two BA cases, but were run after seeing results and do not convert the secondary arm into a primary confirmation or solve site imbalance.

Interpretation limits: the source is still a processed, skull-stripped, MNI-registered volume, not a native voxel reference; 3D Fourier acquisition does not create independent 2D slice measurements. Tumor masks were generated from multiple MRI modalities and reviewed/corrected, not annotated from T2 alone. The 327 thick-2D cases in this dataset would evaluate resampled pseudo-thin targets and cannot rescue the native x4 hypothesis. Existing MRI abnormality-fidelity and T2 interpolation/segmentation work constrains novelty. No GPU or locked IXI test was used and the running terminal training was not touched.

Decision: do not publish the fixed-model tumor-harm generalization from this pilot. Keep HAT as a predeclared secondary lead only; the highest-value next evidence is a second high-resolution T2 source with aligned tumor labels or a prospectively defined downstream endpoint. The prior MIRA released-context audit remains descriptive; Kuestner 2024 methods, clean reproduction, weight rights and author/venue gates remain open.
