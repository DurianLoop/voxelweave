# Frozen analytic-baseline sensitivity for the thin-3D T2 pilot

Written after the SwinIR-primary and HAT-secondary model outcomes were known, before computing any PCHIP values. This is a **post-result sensitivity analysis**, not a new confirmatory primary test. It cannot repair the failed SwinIR pilot or establish clinical harm.

- Use exactly the 33 successfully completed patients and four target slices per patient in `experiments/ds007045_thin3d_model_pilot/cases/*.json`. Keep `sub-NSK47` as the original network failure; do not silently change the original 34-case denominator. Check every local processed T2 and mask against the case-record SHA-256 and the frozen qualification geometry/labels. No new patients, target selection, model inference, download, GPU or locked IXI test.
- For each target `z`, take the four nearest **phase-0 observed** planes at `4(i-1),4i,4(i+1),4(i+2)`, where `i=z//4`. Require all four to exist. Apply SciPy `PchipInterpolator` along the slice axis at `z`, independently per pixel, with no target or phase-2 input. Primary comparator is native-grid/raw PCHIP. Secondary comparator applies the same PCHIP to the model's already defined 256-pixel observed-anchor preprocessing, clips to `[0,1]`, then resizes to the released grid and undoes the observed-only intensity transform. Do not tune clipping or anchor choice after results.
- Copy the exact original tumor (`label>0` intersecting acquired-anchor support) and full-support definitions, target pixels, observed-anchor intensity span and normalized MSE. Check recalculated original raw-linear MSE against archived per-target values to tolerance `1e-6` absolute in intensity-squared units before accepting a target.
- Patient means equally average four target normalized MSEs. Report paired patient-level `HAT-PCHIP` and `SwinIR-PCHIP` for tumor/full support, 5,000 patient bootstrap means and percentile 95% intervals (seed `20261005`), positive case counts, and simultaneous tumor-worse/full-better counts. Retain both raw and pipeline PCHIP comparisons. A positive interval would only show that the **observed secondary HAT deficit is robust to this stronger analytic comparator within this one processed source**. A null or reversed interval weakens that HAT lead. No multiplicity-adjusted or independent-source claim follows.
- Stop and record the case as failed if the local file is missing, a source hash/grid/target check fails, or any metric is nonfinite. Do not replace cases by outcome. Archive per-target and per-patient CSV, summary, script/protocol hashes and failures.
