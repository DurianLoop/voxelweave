# All multi-T2 fastMRI cases: frozen header-only screen

Frozen before reading the selected DICOM headers on 2026-10-01. This is a local source-feasibility audit, not an image experiment. The exact release, access agreement, acquisition chain and publication rights are unresolved. Do not decode pixels or use these cases in the public-only manuscript.

## Selection and immutable input

Use `dataset/fastMRI_brain_T2/processed/metadata/fastmri_brain_t2_series_manifest.csv`, SHA-256 `042730d2802b4b01326d6f297e6bd46e76d5a56d7b044fe60c87d8ccf41c5c2d`. Group rows with `is_t2=True` by `case_id`; select every case with at least two such rows, regardless of `REPEAT` in the label. The expected selection is 61 cases and 138 manifest T2 rows. Read every `.dcm` in each selected case directory under `dataset/fastMRI_brain_DICOM`, including non-T2 series so the series inventory is complete. Refuse a changed manifest, unexpected case count, missing directory, missing DICOM or a file outside the data root. Read with `pydicom.dcmread(..., stop_before_pixels=True)` only; no NIfTI, pixels, weights, locked test or GPU.

Group each case's headers by `SeriesInstanceUID` and enumerate all unordered pairs for which both DICOM `SeriesDescription` values contain `T2` (case insensitive). Report all eligible pairs, including failures; do not choose a winner per case. Each series must have 16 unique physical plane centers and stable orientation, pixel spacing, thickness, TE, TR, frame UID and matrix. Require both `ORIGINAL/PRIMARY`, `MRAcquisitionType=2D`, unique nonempty SOP UIDs within series and disjoint SOP sets between series. Require equal frame UID and matrix, orientation within `1e-4` per component, spacing within `0.01 mm`, thickness within `0.1 mm`, TE within `1 ms`, TR within `10 ms`, and sorted signed plane centers within `0.1 mm`. Missing, unstable or conflicting fields fail the relevant criterion. Record separately (a) centers match, (b) all technical geometry/protocol checks pass, and (c) a usable between-series acquisition or series time difference of at least 1 second exists. A distinct SeriesInstanceUID and timestamp only make a header-level trace-back candidate: they do not prove an independent acquisition event or independently sampled raw planes.

Write only anonymous case ordinals, series ordinals, aggregate counts, scalar maximum center differences, timing differences and reason codes. Never write source case IDs, paths, dates, patient identifiers, raw UIDs, source descriptions or image pixels. Include all failure counts and script/plan/manifest hashes. The previous seven-`REPEAT` scan is a subset, not an independent cohort or confirmation. Even a technical/time candidate cannot enter pixel analysis until the exact source rights, acquisition record, motion/registration controls, target set, weights, endpoints and failure rules are separately documented and frozen.
