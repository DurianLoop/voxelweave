# Local fastMRI repeated-T2 header feasibility gate: frozen before scan

**Purpose:** Determine whether the local fastMRI brain DICOM archive contains technical candidates for two independently recorded T2 series at matched physical slice centers. This is a **header-only feasibility screen**, not a licensed/publication-ready source or a result on patient image pixels. The exact local release/access agreement and image-publication rights remain unverified. Do not add these cases to the public-only manuscript or use locked test images.

## Frozen candidate rule and inputs

Input manifest: `dataset/fastMRI_brain_T2/processed/metadata/fastmri_brain_t2_series_manifest.csv`, SHA-256 `042730d2802b4b01326d6f297e6bd46e76d5a56d7b044fe60c87d8ccf41c5c2d`. Select **all** manifest rows whose `series_label` contains `repeat` case-insensitively and the full DICOM directories of their distinct source cases. Current manifest gives seven cases, each containing 32 or 48 `.dcm` files; refuse unexpected case/file counts rather than silently broadening. Read each DICOM with `pydicom.dcmread(..., stop_before_pixels=True)`. No `pixel_array`, NIfTI or GPU operations.

For each case, group all available DICOM headers by `SeriesInstanceUID`. Record only anonymous case ordinals and aggregate technical fields: series-label class, count, `MRAcquisitionType`, full `ImageType`, orientation, pixel spacing, slice thickness, per-plane image position, acquisition/series times and UID uniqueness **counts**. Do not write source case IDs, paths, raw UIDs, patient names or image pixels to the result. Verify 16 unique planes for a candidate series and stable orientation/pixel spacing within it. Calculate signed physical layer coordinates by projecting `ImagePositionPatient` onto the normal from `ImageOrientationPatient`; compare sorted coordinates between series with a `0.1 mm` max absolute difference, orientation components within `1e-4`, pixel-spacing components within `0.01 mm`, and slice thickness within `0.1 mm`. Require matching `FrameOfReferenceUID`, image rows/columns, echo time within `1 ms` and repetition time within `10 ms`; missing values fail a full protocol match. Require different SeriesInstanceUID, unique SOPInstanceUID sets and reported distinct acquisition or series times to call them *header-compatible repeated-series candidates*. Treat missing or conflicting metadata as failure, not a match. Count possible pairs among T2-labeled series for every selected case, regardless of whether the repeated series has an obvious base `AX T2` label.

ImageType `ORIGINAL/PRIMARY` and `MRAcquisitionType=2D` are necessary descriptive evidence but do not prove raw independently sampled k-space planes. The label `REPEAT` is not sufficient. Even a header-compatible pair needs official exact release/access authorization, acquisition chain documentation and motion/registration controls before a scientific acquisition-identity experiment. Any future target-pixel work must first freeze its own patient/series target list, model contract, endpoint and failures; this screen cannot preregister that later analysis by itself.
