# MRI innovation candidates after the bounded literature and evidence audit

Scope: single-T2 x4 missing-slice reconstruction and the related MIRA measurement audit. This memo synthesizes the [157-title citation-neighbour screen](literature/2026-10-01_mira_citation_neighbour_result.md), [focused full-text neighbours](literature/2026-10-01_shared_context_neighbours_fulltext.md), [NPB-REC full-text check](literature/2026-10-01_npb_rec_fulltext_result.md), [Edupuganti full-text check](literature/2026-10-01_edupuganti_fulltext_result.md), [Savant 2026 full-text check](literature/2026-10-01_savant_formulation_fulltext_result.md), prior [Hu 2021 comparison](literature/2026-09-30_hu_mira_overlap.md), [third-source UPENN result](UPENN_GBM_PROCESSED_T2_CONTEXT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), and the current [publication gate](CLAIM_PUBLICATION_GATE_2026-09-30.md). It is a decision aid, **not** a systematic review or a priority certificate. No new GPU run or locked IXI test inspection was used.

The practical next decision is therefore **publication framing versus new physical source**. Candidate 1 now has three separately released public strata, but only one fixed IXI pair and a later-informed third-source protocol; novelty, rights and clean reproduction gates remain open. Candidate 2 is potentially more explanatory, yet no current source passes its causal comparison. Adding 374 processed UPENN patients strengthens empirical breadth, not native-plane provenance or a deployable score. UPENN's paired MSE was slightly worse under disjoint context, so the `O` effect must not be advertised as reconstruction improvement.

**Forward-citation and clinical-task update (2026-10-01).** The [four-seed citation audit](literature/2026-10-01_four_seed_forward_citation_result.md) screened 62 indexed records/61 distinct work IDs and read two accessible arXiv HTML full texts. *Mind the Detail* tested pathology recall and mAP on fastMRI+ knee under a fixed undersampled k-space input; *Evaluating Structural Uncertainty* measured segmentation volume bias/variance under fixed 5x T1 k-space inputs. Neither read protocol is the released-neighbor same-target switch, but both make a clinical-consequence claim based only on `Delta O` untenable. Segmentation-Guided Reconstruction, a unified MoDL uncertainty paper, a 2026 brain-abnormality study, and Kuestner 2024 remain abstract-level comparators; their methods cannot be certified non-overlapping. The [MSValid ZIP label inventory and frozen future-task gate](CLINICAL_TASK_LABEL_SOURCE_GATE_2026-10-01.md) found 398 modality NIfTI members and no label file in that official ZIP. No lesion metric was run. Priority 1 remains a narrow empirical audit; the next material upgrades are a verified same-plane clinical label source or genuine matched independent acquisition, subject to separate gates. Another unannotated positive cohort would add less to reviewability.

**Official UPENN label-space correction and manuscript update.** The [TCIA source-page check](UPENN_GBM_LABEL_ALIGNMENT_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) confirms expert-corrected tumor labels exist, but its submitting group explicitly warns that atlas-registered NIfTI/segmentation and DICOM do not align by design. This fails the immediate label gate for all 1,496 frozen DICOM targets; a patient-name join would not establish a valid transform. A new aligned NIfTI study would be a distinct protocol. The [internal English manuscript](PUBLIC_ONLY_MIRA_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md) now contains the third-source table, formal UPENN data citation, two full-text task-aware comparators and an anonymized [three-source patient figure](figures/PUBLIC_THREE_SOURCE_FIGURE_NOTE_2026-10-01.md). Its old five-page PDF is explicitly a two-source snapshot. This improves reviewability of the existing narrow claim, but original-acquisition causality, clinical consequence, Kuestner methods-level novelty and submission rights remain open.

**Feasible next source, not yet an experiment.** The [official UPENN CSV screen](UPENN_GBM_NIFTI_LABEL_FEASIBILITY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found 232 distinct baseline scan IDs marked corrected-tumor-label available, with 114 subject prefixes also appearing among the frozen DICOM patients. The official NIfTI/label package is approximately 69 GB and uses an Aspera access route; no files or pixels have been obtained. This is enough to define a conditional *new atlas-NIfTI* tumor-region experiment after exact T2/mask grid and scan-ID verification, but not enough to ascribe masks to the DICOM results. Priority 1 remains a narrow published-context audit; a task-relevant extension should be pursued on the aligned release only after its own frozen eligibility and equal-information controls. Continuing to accumulate unlabeled DICOM positives has lower scientific value.

**Thin-grid pilot outcome.** Among those corrected-label baseline metadata rows, 86 have a T2 thickness near 0.9 mm and 83 have a matching exact-description NBIA T2 SAG SPACE processed series. The [preselected first-series nonpixel check](UPENN_GBM_THIN_T2_HEADER_PILOT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found a uniform 0.9 mm physical 3D grid but all 192 images marked DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER, with no source-image chain. Therefore this is a potentially useful *processed thin-grid* task source, not a verified independently acquired thin reference. Do not reopen candidate 2's original-acquisition identity claim on the basis of this nominal thickness.

**New full-text novelty pressure.** The [targeted recent-paper audit](literature/2026-10-01_targeted_recent_fulltext_result.md) read SliceBridge's 13-page preprint and a 27-page task-aware glioma UQ paper. SliceBridge already tests bilateral slice-context restoration, IXI external evaluation, through-plane consistency and a downstream morphometry endpoint; its interval-coupling consistency advantage did not significantly beat matched uncoupled flow on the downstream ROI metric. The glioma paper already tests uncertainty against segmentation errors and selective prediction. Candidate 1 therefore retains only its narrow fixed-reference, switched-companion paired-error estimand. Candidate 3's generic context-consistency/missing-slice framing and any task-risk first claim are weaker. Neither paper supplies the missing original-acquisition comparison or label alignment for the existing DICOM result; Kuestner remains abstract-only and the UPENN atlas package inventory is still absent.

**Three-source fixed-denominator closure.** The [post-result UPENN sensitivity](UPENN_FIXED_DENOMINATOR_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) keeps each target's shared paired MSE in both denominators and returns +28.4961 points (374/374 patients, 1,496/1,496 targets positive), matching the earlier MSValid/ReMIND control's direction. This rules out denominator change as the sole arithmetic explanation of the third-source contrast. It does not make `O` actionable, establish native acquisition identity, or improve paired reconstruction MSE.

**Official NIfTI file gate and older T2 task precedent.** The [Faspex public-link inventory](UPENN_NIFTI_OFFICIAL_PACKAGE_INVENTORY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) now confirms 671 structural scan directories but only 147 candidate corrected-segmentation file links among 232 CSV-marked baseline scans; 60 of those 147 have approximately 0.9 mm T2 thickness metadata. One thin candidate has exact-ID T2 and segmentation link names, yet no NIfTI headers were transferred, so actual file validity and atlas-grid pairing remain open. The public configuration has no HTTP gateway and this machine lacks Aspera Connect. [Verschuur 2022 full text](literature/2026-10-01_verschuur_neonatal_t2_fulltext_result.md) shows a prior T2 slice-interpolation/segmentation study with explicit adjacent-motion failures and volume bias. Any future task-level claim must beat a same-information cubic baseline and cannot claim first T2 interpolation to improve segmentation. Candidate 1 retains only the narrow paired-error observability contrast; candidate 2 still lacks native acquisition identity.

**Subsequent access and task-aware prior-art update.** An IBM-signed portable Aspera CLI was obtained without system installation; its [exact two-file pilot](UPENN_ASPERA_TWO_FILE_ACCESS_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) reached token authentication and returned exit 1, with zero NIfTI bytes. Thus the preceding "lacks Aspera Connect" statement describes the earlier inventory stage; same-grid labels remain unproven. [Morshuis et al. 2024 full text](literature/2026-10-01_segmentation_guided_mri_fulltext_result.md) adds a task-aware accelerated-MRI precedent: on SKM-TEA knee, guided diffusion reconstructions bracket segmentation volume more effectively than repeated sampling from the same k-space input. It does not implement this project's published-neighbor intervention, but any novelty wording around reconstruction diversity revealing segmentation uncertainty is now ruled out. Priority 1 remains a narrow retrospective measurement audit; a tumor-region claim still requires an independently frozen atlas-space experiment.

[Fischer, Kuestner and Baumgartner 2023 full text](literature/2026-10-01_fischer_mri_uq_fulltext_result.md) is an earlier, closer task-error precedent: 20 sampled reconstructions from the same undersampled SKM-TEA knee input are segmented, and segmentation variance maps are compared against error maps across acceleration shifts. This also leaves the exact released-neighbor MIRA contrast untested, but definitively removes any claim that propagation of MRI reconstruction disagreement to segmentation error is new. It does not supply lesion labels for the current DICOM cohort, and the later Kuestner 2024 paper still needs its own methods read.

**Frozen annotated-region test completed.** The official MSD BrainTumour registered-release [result](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) attempted all 484 training pairs, completed 483 patients/1,932 frozen tumor-positive targets, and passed the two jointly required tests: tumor `Delta O=+11.6537` points (95% patient CI +11.2256 to +12.0681) and tumor-minus-same-slice local label-negative `Delta O=+0.5819` points (CI +0.2302 to +0.9337). Independent arithmetic/denominator verification passed. The latter effect is modest and positive in 267/483 patients. Same-observation PCHIP had lower tumor MSE than the disjoint deep-model average in 482/483 patients; patient-mean MSE was 236.57 versus 1,208.05. This **strengthens only the narrow processed-release, task-region measurement claim**; it decisively fails to justify a new reconstruction-performance or clinical-benefit claim. MSD/BRaTS anonymous cases are not proven disjoint from other TCIA-derived sources. Candidate 1 remains retrospective and source/model-specific, while candidate 2's native-acquisition identity and candidate 3's target-response truth gates remain open. Kuestner 2024 full-text novelty, rights and clean-reproduction gates still prevent a publication-ready declaration.

**Analytic falsification narrows candidate 1 further.** The [post-result PCHIP0/linear phase-switch control](MSD_TASK01_ANALYTIC_CONTEXT_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) completed the same 483 cases and independently verified all four target/ROI joins. It produced tumor-minus-local `Delta O=+0.9428` points (exploratory patient CI +0.5503 to +1.3381), versus the neural result's +0.5819; the paired difference CI includes zero. A conventional analytic pair thus reproduces a small positive region contrast under the released-neighbor switch. Do not claim that the MSD finding reveals a deep-architecture-specific tumor mechanism or that architectural novelty follows from the label result. The evidence supports a descriptive context-dependence audit; stronger causal or task claims require a new source/experiment.

**Kirby21 original-acquisition source screen.** The [paper/official-source gate](KIRBY21_REPEAT_T2_SOURCE_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) finds a repeated T2-weighted DTI geometric-reference sequence in the shared short/extended protocol, but it is a 3D Fourier acquisition with nominal 2 mm acquired resolution; the reported 1 mm overlapping sagittal slices do not establish independent 2D acquisitions. Forty-two official session links and a public HEAD do not prove each subject's file-level pair or native image geometry. BIRN use terms were read, not accepted on the user's behalf. Candidate 2 remains blocked for the project's original 2D slice-identity test; no image bundle or pixel experiment was started.

**ON-Harmony source intersection check.** A [full-text plus official nonimage-metadata audit](ON_HARMONY_REPEAT_T2_SOURCE_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) shows that 2D T2w FLAIR was acquired on GE MR750, whereas none of the nine participants' within-scanner repeat codes is MR750. Thus a recent openly licensed travelling-heads dataset still cannot supply candidate 2's same-scanner 2D pair. The [145-work targeted metadata screen](literature/2026-10-01_native_2d_t2_targeted_search_result.md) produced no immediately qualifying source and does not close novelty. Candidate 1 remains the strongest narrow empirical result, not yet publication-ready.

**2015 multimodal retest full-text correction.** The [57-person paper's protocol](CONNECTIVITY_TEST_RETEST_T2_SOURCE_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) places T2w structural imaging only in session 1; the repeated S-MRI is T1w 3D MP-RAGE. This lead is closed at paper-protocol level, subject to the stated limit that no archive members were inspected. It supplies no candidate 2 pair.

**Two-source analytic falsification changes the novelty assessment.** A [frozen post-result PCHIP0/linear phase-switch test](TWO_SOURCE_ANALYTIC_CONTEXT_FALSIFICATION_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) completed all original 727 MSValid/ReMIND targets and produced +29.2470/+30.9585 point patient-equal `Delta O`, positive in all 146 patients. The original neural contrasts were +24.1856/+26.3999 points on these same targets. Independent formula/patient/bootstrap checks and two source-pixel replays passed. The two estimator pairs have unequal marginal errors, so their numerical gap is not an identified architecture effect; nevertheless, a large context contrast plainly does not require learned architectures. Candidate 1 should be described as a retrospective released-context measurement, with weaker methodological novelty than the earlier table suggests. It is **not yet a publishable claim** without a sharper independent consequence and full prior-art/reproducibility gates.

**Equal-neighbor performance control changes the method decision.** In the [post-result MSD join](MSD_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_LINEAR_AUDIT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), raw two-phase-0-neighbor linear achieved patient-mean tumor MSE 805.23 versus HAT0 1,597.15 and SwinIR0 1,403.28 on the same 483 cases/1,932 targets. A [post-first-case-pilot CPU replay](MSD_PIPELINE_LINEAR_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) matched the model's min/max and resize/clip path without its residual; that pipeline linear had tumor MSE 1,068.26, still below both models by +528.89/+335.02 MSE. This is a strong negative baseline for the fixed IXI→registered-MSD deployment, not proof of architecture- or tumor-specific causal harm. Local label-negative differences are smaller and SwinIR beats pipeline linear there. Existing papers already cover abnormality degradation, linear interpolation and selective restoration actions; [focused audit](literature/2026-10-01_msd_linear_failure_prior_art.md). Do not pursue a new model or write a performance claim until an IXI-validation-only comparison, aligned independent pathology source and strict equal-processing baseline are established. Candidate 1 stays a descriptive measurement audit; candidate 2 and all publication gates remain open.

**Whole-foreground falsifier prevents a global-collapse claim.** Archived-CSV [MSValid/ReMIND whole-support joins](TWO_SOURCE_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_PERFORMANCE_CHECK_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) show both IXI models below raw-linear MSE in every completed patient (84/84 and 62/62) in each source's own intensity units. The subsequently frozen [MSD whole-support pixel replay](MSD_FULL_SUPPORT_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_POSTRESULT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) finds HAT minus raw linear -20.12 MSE (interval -140.48 to +96.99) and SwinIR -221.40 (-350.15 to -102.88), despite their strong tumor-ROI deficits. Within the same MSD case, 195 HAT and 197 SwinIR patients have a positive tumor excess while their global excess is negative. This is a more precise negative result: full-image scores can obscure local annotated-region error for these models. It still has direct abnormality-fidelity prior art and lacks independent aligned labels or a deployable predictor; no novel safety mechanism is established.

**IXI validation diagnostic and UCSF source gate.** The [58-case IXI validation-only CPU replay](IXI_VALIDATION_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) completed 232 frozen targets: HAT/SwinIR beat raw linear in 57/58 patients each, with patient-mean MSE differences -1,923.06/-1,853.99. This rejects a universal code-path failure but is development-set evidence without tumor masks, so it does not identify the cause of the MSD tumor deficit. The [UCSF-PDGM source audit](UCSF_PDGM_INDEPENDENT_T2_LABEL_SOURCE_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) finds 495 people/501 exams, six repeat exams and 298 BraTS21-mapped exams in v5 metadata. The remaining 203 unmapped exams are not proven disjoint from MSD or model development. A [read-only file-list pilot](UCSF_PDGM_SINGLE_CASE_FILE_LIST_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) matched all 501 public package directories to CSV IDs and found exact T2/mask symbolic links in one preselected unmapped case, but neither file contents nor affine has been checked. UCSF is only a possible processed-grid confirmation cohort. Candidate 1 remains a retrospective measurement audit, while a pathology generalization or new residual-fallback method remains unqualified. Kuestner 2024 methods, independent aligned labels, IXI-weight rights, clean reproduction and author/venue review are still open publication gates.

**UCSF access does not yet close the independent-label gate.** The [official ascli two-file pilot](UCSF_PDGM_ASCLI_TWO_FILE_ACCESS_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) verified a digest-pinned IBM portable client, public-link browse and exact-two-file transfer specifications. Two bounded CLI receive forms both produced Faspex HTTP 500 and zero NIfTI bytes. This is an access failure with unresolved server/plugin cause, not evidence against label validity or a reason to use the whole 142-GB package. Header shape/affine and identity overlap remain open; neither the MSD tumor result nor a new method can be externally confirmed from UCSF yet.

**OpenNeuro ds007045 upgrades source feasibility, not the claim.** A [three-paper full-text and official-source audit](literature/2026-10-01_three_glioma_label_sources_result.md) ranks the Filimonova et al. preoperative GBM set above UCSD-PTGBM (partly BraTS-overlapping, postoperative and TCIA-mediated) and LUMIERE (automatic voxel masks). Official S3/GraphQL metadata show that the paper's v1.0.1/337 and current CC0 v2.0.1/363 are different cohorts; current README 2018-2025 and paper methods 2024-2025 also conflict. The paper's pretrained nnUNet versus SegResNet segmentation descriptions need clarification. One v2.0.1 processed T2/mask pair passed identical `182 x 218 x 182` and affine headers, and a [frozen one-case CPU linear pilot](DS007045_BA3_CPU_PILOT_RESULT_2026-10-01.json) computed lesion/nonlesion MAE on 135 missing targets without using a model. Its tumor-region MAE was lower, not higher, than non-tumor brain (0.00650 versus 0.01817), an anatomically confounded single-case observation. The useful next innovation test is **independent processed-grid replication of MSD's patient-level global-benefit/lesion-deficit sign discordance**, with fixed model, equal-observation/processing linear comparator and a pinned cohort. Neither this pilot nor the source's mask makes clinical consequence or original-acquisition causality established. Candidate 1 remains descriptive and not publication-ready.

**Current-cohort denominator is now concrete.** The [official S3 key audit](literature/source_cache/ds007045_current_pair_audit_2026-10-01/result.json) finds 363/363 distinct current-v2.0.1 processed T2/mask filename pairs across eight site codes, no missing or duplicate pair keys. This closes only file-list completeness. Shape/affine checks beyond BA3, mask quality, version-pinned snapshots and overlap with IXI/MSD/BraTS remain unverified; the future clinical replication must not promote 363 file pairs into 363 eligible evaluated patients without those gates.

**Acquisition screen overturns the naive 363-case follow-up.** The [full official sidecar/geometry audit](DS007045_SOURCE_QUALIFICATION_AND_ACQUISITION_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) found 359 exact-grid label-qualified pairs but 327/363 original T2 sequences are 2D with >=2 mm slice thickness (4 mm in 228). Only 34 are metadata-defined high-resolution 3D T2, 31 from one site. The earlier BA3 linear pilot used raw 5-mm slices with 6-mm spacing resampled into a 1-mm MNI volume; it cannot represent native thin-slice recovery. The proposed full-363 model protocol is held for this task rather than treating processed-grid targets as acquired truth. Four mask/T2 affine mismatches remain excluded.

**Primary high-resolution 3D replication is negative.** The [predeclared 34-case CPU pilot](DS007045_THIN3D_MODEL_PILOT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) completed 33 cases/132 targets (one source timeout) and passed independent source-hash/arithmetic checks. Against model-pipeline linear, SwinIR's patient-mean normalized tumor MSE difference was -0.00000340 (95% bootstrap interval -0.00004633 to +0.00004103), while full-support difference was -0.00078717 (interval entirely negative). Thus the prewritten requirement of a mean tumor deficit plus global benefit **failed**, despite 14/33 sign-discordant individuals. HAT, a prespecified secondary comparison, showed tumor +0.00015243 (interval entirely positive) and full -0.00079957 (entirely negative), a model-specific lead that cannot be promoted to primary confirmation on these same data. The 30/33 NSK concentration, registered 3D truth and multimodal labels still prevent a publishable clinical/general claim. The next source must be genuinely high-resolution and independent; adding thick-2D pseudo-thin targets would weaken validity.

**Second-source targeted screen has not supplied confirmation.** [IMAGO and EGD full-text/source audit](literature/2026-10-01_second_highres_t2_glioma_source_screen.md) finds IMAGO's 500-person T2 explicitly 2D multislice despite 1-mm MNI release, and EGD's 374 manual masks promising but original T2 resolution unqualified; its official XNAT subject list currently returns HTTP 403. No access term was accepted. Neither is an immediately valid 3D T2 replication cohort, so HAT remains a single-source secondary signal and the publication goal remains open.
