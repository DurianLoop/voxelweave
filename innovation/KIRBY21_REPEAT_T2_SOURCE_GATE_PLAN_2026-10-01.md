# Kirby21 repeated-T2 official-source screen

Frozen before any Kirby21 source request beyond general bibliography knowledge. Candidate: Landman et al.'s multi-modal MRI reproducibility resource, often called Kirby21. First resolve the exact paper DOI and publisher data citation from OpenAlex/Crossref, then inspect the official NITRC resource page and its file inventory for repeated **T2-weighted structural** measurements. Use direct public HTTP with bounded response sizes, archive exact URLs and response hashes. Do not obtain patient image bytes in this stage.

A usable original-acquisition identity cohort would require more than a study title: two distinct acquisition records per subject; T2w rather than T1w, FLAIR or quantitative T2 maps; same scanner and protocol or documented covariates; usable acquisition timestamps; matching native slice orientation/center, in-plane grid and layer thickness; explicit lawful research/publication terms; and sufficient eligible independent patients. Exact same-plane geometry may fail even in a test-retest dataset. If official metadata or access cannot establish these points, record the failure and stop before pixels or model inference. No GPU, locked IXI test or running training process may be touched.
