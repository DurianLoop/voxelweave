# Follow-up queue after the same-neighbor MSD negative baseline

Current result: [483-case audit](MSD_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_LINEAR_AUDIT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md). Each item below is a new experiment, not an interpretation already established by the existing patient bootstrap. Preserve the 484-case attempt denominator and `BRATS_044` failure in any MSD extension; avoid the locked IXI test and the running GPU task.

The result-informed [MSValid/ReMIND full-support CSV check](TWO_SOURCE_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_PERFORMANCE_CHECK_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) and [MSD exact full-support replay](MSD_FULL_SUPPORT_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_POSTRESULT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) are now complete. They reject a proposed whole-image MSD failure interpretation: the strongest observed contrast is tumor ROI versus full foreground within the same MSD cases.

1. **Completed CPU control:** the [post-pilot frozen full replay](MSD_PIPELINE_LINEAR_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) compared raw linear0, pipeline-linear0, HAT0 and SwinIR0 on all existing 483 complete cases and both ROIs. Tumor MSEs were 805.23, 1,068.26, 1,597.15 and 1,403.28. The image pipeline explains a descriptive +263.03 MSE of the raw-linear gap, but both models remain worse than pipeline linear in tumor. This does not isolate a causal model-prior contribution.
2. **Completed validation-only diagnostic:** the [58-case CPU result](IXI_VALIDATION_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) scored four fixed targets per case. HAT/SwinIR improve whole-support MSE over raw linear by 1,923.06/1,853.99 in the patient mean, each in 57/58 validation cases. The raw and pipeline linear means are nearly identical on native 256-square IXI images. This rules out a code path that always loses to linear, but validation selected the checkpoints and has no matched tumor ROI; it is not independent confirmation or causal evidence for the MSD contrast.
3. **CPU/source gate: independent T2 pathology cohort.** Obtain exact T2/label space and permissions for the separate UPENN atlas NIfTI package or another official source. Without a second correctly paired annotated source, no tumor-generalization statement is justified.
