Average two targets per residue, residues equally per patient, and patients equally across the 483 complete cases. Report raw/pipeline/HAT/SwinIR full-support MSE and HAT/SwinIR minus raw and pipeline means, positive/negative/tied patient counts, and 5,000 patient-bootstrap percentile intervals with seed `20261011`. Directly compare only the *sign* of model-minus-raw full-support estimates with the existing MSValid/ReMIND full-support archived-CSV result; never pool raw MSE magnitudes across cohorts or claim a causal domain effect.
