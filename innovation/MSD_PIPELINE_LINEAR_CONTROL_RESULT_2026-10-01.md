# Full CPU replay: model preprocessing versus raw two-neighbor linear

The [full-run protocol](MSD_PIPELINE_LINEAR_CONTROL_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) was fixed **after** one `BRATS_001` pilot showed a raw/pipeline-linear difference and before any of the other 482 complete cases were calculated. This is a post-result explanatory control, not an independent test. The [script](experiments/msd_pipeline_linear_full_control.py) replayed both exact official local compressed members for all 483 complete MSD Task01 cases, validated their source SHA-256s against the earlier archives, reconstructed all 1,932 original raw-linear target/ROI MSEs within `1e-6`, and retained `BRATS_044` in the 484-attempt denominator as the pre-existing label-transfer failure. It never downloaded data, loaded a checkpoint, used GPU or opened the locked IXI test. The [full summary](experiments/msd_pipeline_linear_full_control/summary.json), [patient CSV](experiments/msd_pipeline_linear_full_control/per_patient.csv), [target-region CSV](experiments/msd_pipeline_linear_full_control/per_target_region.csv) and [first-case pixel pilot](experiments/msd_pipeline_linear_preflight_brats001.json) are archived.

All intervals are 5,000 patient-bootstrap percentile intervals with seed `20261008`, conditional on this post-pilot model/cohort choice. In the tumor ROI, the cohort-mean pipeline-linear excess over raw linear is 33.2% of HAT's and 44.0% of SwinIR's raw-linear excess. These are ratios of descriptive mean differences, **not causal mediation fractions**. The model residual, output clipping, training data and image pipeline interact. The first-case pilot had an unusually large pipeline effect (468.49 to 915.93 tumor MSE), which is why the all-case result was required. Independent PowerShell aggregation of the 483-row CSV reproduced the key full-cohort means (+263.0311, +528.8862 and +335.0189) and the 451/417 positive counts; 3,864 target-region rows represent exactly two ROIs on 1,932 targets, with no negative MSE.

The 240-to-256 resize and resize-back pipeline clearly degrades the raw linear baseline on this processed release, yet it does **not** account for the entire tumor deficit of either network. Local label-negative behavior differs: HAT is close to pipeline linear, while SwinIR improves on it in the patient mean. This pattern makes an added residual that behaves differently in the annotated tumor region a testable hypothesis, but the present MSEs cannot say whether the cause is pathology content, intensity/texture distribution, training shift, registration or label selection. The region comparison is not a T2-only clinical task. A tumor-specific safety or deployment claim would require an acquired-only predictor, independent annotated source and downstream diagnostic/segmentation validation.

The [focused literature note](literature/2026-10-01_msd_linear_failure_prior_art.md) rules out broad originality around abnormality degradation, analytic interpolation baselines or choosing restoration actions. The strongest immediate use is to correct the project's own MIRA performance narrative and define a fair next benchmark. The [follow-up queue](MSD_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_FAILURE_FOLLOWUP_2026-10-01.md) now places an in-domain IXI *validation-only* check and a second aligned pathology source ahead of any new model training.
