# MSD BrainTumour all-pair nonpixel source gate

The [frozen full-header rule](MSD_TASK01_FULL_HEADER_QUALIFICATION_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) applied to all 484 exact training pairs in the [official tar index](MSD_TASK01_OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md). The [initial report](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/all_training_pair_headers.json) found **483/484** image/label pairs with exact `240 x 240 x 155` spatial dimensions, four image channels, the specified types/zooms, nonzero qform/sform and matched affine. The only initial failure, `BRATS_230`, was a TLS unexpected EOF before any header was recorded; no malformed NIfTI or geometry mismatch was observed.

The [pre-retry amendment](MSD_TASK01_HEADER_RETRY_AMENDMENT_2026-10-01.md) restricted a retry to that same case and unchanged header criteria. Its [separate result](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/brats230_header_transport_retry.json) passed. An [independent read-only verification](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/header_verification.json) recalculated exact path joins, source/protocol hashes and all stored header criteria: **484/484 effective same-release-grid pairs**. This exceeds the preregistered 450-pair minimum, without replacing a case or changing tolerance. Only bounded compressed prefixes were requested; no patient image or label voxel was decoded, and no GPU or locked IXI test data was used.

The result is a strong **file and release-space** eligibility gate for the separately [frozen tumor-ROI experiment](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md), not a pathology outcome. Registered BRaTS 1 mm affines are not native-acquisition spacing evidence. Multi-modality segmentation masks do not by themselves establish T2-only diagnostic performance or validate masks on any previously studied DICOM release.
