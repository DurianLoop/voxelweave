# Post-result analytic context control for the MSD tumor-region finding

**Status: frozen after reading the complete HAT/SwinIR MSD result, before computing any analytic-control prediction or ROI result.** This is an exploratory falsification analysis, not an independent confirmation or an amendment to the two original primary tests. The question is whether the small positive tumor-minus-local `Delta O` can arise with ordinary interpolation under the same published-neighbor phase switch, without learned-model architecture.

Use exactly the 484 sorted official Task01_BrainTumour training case IDs, the existing [label qualification](experiments/msd_task01_label_qualification.json), its four target slices per eligible case, and the exact acquired-phase-0 support, tumor ROI and equal-area nearest label-negative ROI from the [original frozen protocol](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md). Keep `BRATS_044` and any later source/metric failure in the 484 denominator. Require the already archived exact official compressed members; do not download new data or alter the source files. Verify source bytes, NIfTI headers, target IDs and masks with the existing source reader. No learned inference, GPU or training.

For each selected target `z`, let `i=floor(z/4)`. The fixed analytic reference `a` is SciPy PCHIP at `z` from four phase-0 real planes at `4(i-1),4i,4(i+1),4(i+2)`. The shared partner `b0` is raw-intensity linear interpolation from the middle phase-0 planes `4i,4(i+1)`. The disjoint partner `b2` is raw-intensity linear interpolation from its two bracketing phase-2 real planes. Compute interpolation and metrics in float64 after decoding the published float32 T2 pixels; construct the support and ROIs on the decoded float32 planes exactly as in the original experiment. Both conditions compare with the same withheld T2 target `y` and the same ROI. The original HAT/SwinIR metric formula applies separately within tumor and local control: `T=(MSE(a,y)+MSE(b,y))/2`, `O=mean((a-b)^2)/(4T)`, `Delta O=O(a,b2)-O(a,b0)`. Reject nonfinite or nonpositive denominators rather than impute. First average two targets within each residue, then the two residues equally, then complete patients equally.

Report the 484 attempted case count, completed/failed IDs, both analytic patient-equal `Delta O` means, tumor-minus-local mean, positive-patient counts and 5,000-draw paired patient-bootstrap percentile intervals (seed `20261006`). Compare this analytic contrast with the already archived neural contrast on the intersection of complete IDs; report the paired difference and its patient-bootstrap interval using the same draws. Also report raw disagreement MSE, paired MSE and a fixed shared-denominator `Delta O` to expose metric arithmetic. There is **no post-result success threshold**. A positive analytic tumor-minus-local contrast weakens any claim that the regional effect depends on deep architectures. A null or negative analytic contrast does not establish a neural mechanism because the analytic estimators have different marginal errors and use a different fixed reference. Neither outcome licenses clinical, native-acquisition or deployment claims.
