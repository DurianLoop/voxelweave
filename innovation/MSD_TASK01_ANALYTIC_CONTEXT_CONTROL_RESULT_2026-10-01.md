# Analytic interpolation falsification of the MSD tumor-region context effect

This [protocol](MSD_TASK01_ANALYTIC_CONTEXT_CONTROL_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) was written **after** the complete neural MSD result was known, but before any analytic-control predictions were computed. It is exploratory falsification, not an independent confirmatory cohort. The [CPU-only case run](experiments/msd_task01_analytic_context_control) attempted all 484 official training cases, completed the same 483 patients and 1,932 targets as the [neural result](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), and retained `BRATS_044` as the single prior label-transfer failure. No new image download, model inference, GPU or locked IXI test access occurred. The independent [case/formula/bootstrap verifier](experiments/verify_msd_task01_analytic_context_control.py) returned `verified: true` and checked exact target IDs, all tumor/local ROI pixel counts and run-contract hashes against the neural case files.

The fixed analytic reference is four phase-0-anchor PCHIP. Its shared companion is phase-0 linear interpolation; its disjoint companion is phase-2 linear interpolation. All use the same published T2 target planes and original acquired-only ROIs. The [complete summary](experiments/msd_task01_analytic_context_control/summary.json) reports patient-equal estimates with 5,000 patient-bootstrap draws, seed `20261006`:

| Contrast | Analytic estimate | 95% patient CI | Positive patients |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tumor `Delta O` | +29.9962 percentage points | +29.5800 to +30.4183 | 483/483 |
| Same-slice equal-area local label-negative `Delta O` | +29.0534 points | +28.6911 to +29.4472 | Descriptive |
| Tumor minus local `Delta O` | **+0.9428 points** | **+0.5503 to +1.3381** | 296/483 |
| Neural minus analytic tumor-minus-local, same 483 patients | -0.3609 points | -0.7910 to +0.0761 | Descriptive paired comparison |

The analytic tumor-minus-local contrast is positive and of the same small order as the neural pair's +0.5819 points. Its point estimate is larger, but the paired difference interval crosses zero. Therefore the observed MSD regional contrast **does not support a deep-architecture-specific mechanism**. A generic published-plane context switch between standard interpolation rules can yield this pattern. This does not prove that all of the neural effect has the same cause: the analytic estimators have different marginal errors, four phase-0 reference anchors and raw-intensity float64 interpolation, so a controlled architecture-isolation experiment has not been done.

The [paired-error component table](experiments/msd_task01_analytic_context_control/metrics_descriptive.json) gives patient-equal lesion shared/disjoint `O` of 7.9700%/37.9662% and local shared/disjoint `O` of 3.9921%/33.0455%. Lesion raw squared output disagreement rises from 124.19 to 1,031.27; local disagreement rises from 226.17 to 3,449.71. Region-specific paired MSEs and the fixed-shared-denominator sensitivity are archived in the summary and component table. The changed denominator is not the only arithmetic term; phase-2 adds real observations relative to a phase-0-only x4 input and alters anatomy/context together with measurement identity.

This result should be cited as a **negative novelty control** alongside the original tumor-region result. It strengthens the caution that the released-context observability effect is broad and can be reproduced by simple interpolation, while narrowing any method-specific claim. It supplies no lesion-detection, segmentation, calibration, patient-safety, original-acquisition or reconstruction-performance endpoint. The original neural MSD result remains statistically positive under its frozen contract, but its scientific contribution is now more clearly a retrospective measurement audit rather than a new learned reconstruction mechanism.
