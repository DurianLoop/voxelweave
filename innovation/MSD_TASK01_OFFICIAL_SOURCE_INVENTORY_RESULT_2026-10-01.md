# Official Medical Segmentation Decathlon BrainTumour source inventory

The [AWS Open Data Registry page](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/registry.html) identifies the Medical Segmentation Decathlon resource as managed by the MONAI Development Team, licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0 International, and cited as the Medical Segmentation Decathlon accessed from the registry. The public S3 listing and uncompressed `Task01_BrainTumour.tar` are archived by URL, size, ETag and response hashes in the [complete tar-header index](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/tar_index_full.json). The tar is 7,608,266,240 bytes. An HTTP Range request returned exact 206 byte spans. The indexer read only 512-byte tar headers plus the small `dataset.json`; it did **not** decompress image or label voxels, download the full tar, or use the GPU.

The official [dataset.json](literature/source_cache/msd_task01_official_2026-10-01/dataset.json), SHA-256 `77f38db2fcf1a39ad4923f482f30c948cc7349fa7823cd307ceb3145adb81412`, describes the 2018 release as BRATS: four channels `0=FLAIR`, `1=T1w`, `2=t1gd`, `3=T2w`, with background, edema, non-enhancing tumor and enhancing tumor labels. It lists 484 paired training cases and 266 test images. All **484/484 exact image paths**, **484/484 exact label paths** and **266/266 exact test paths** occur in the complete tar index, with no duplicate training path or missing member. The extra directory entries and `._` macOS sidecars are not clinical cases. Training image member sizes range from 5,934,697 to 13,393,645 bytes; label members range from 11,765 to 62,922 bytes. No training image was decoded to obtain these counts.

This source is a new **registered/preprocessed 3D release**, not an original native-acquisition record. Its tumor masks are a plausible same-release task label source for a separate T2 channel study, but exact image/label shape and affine, target-plane label positivity, preprocessing lineage and real acquisition slice spacing remain unverified until the staged header and voxel checks. The source may overlap other BRaTS/TCIA patient collections; anonymous case IDs alone cannot prove cross-dataset independence. It cannot repair the original-acquisition identity gate or be retrofitted to the existing processed-UPENN DICOM targets.

The [pre-pixel header gate](MSD_TASK01_HEADER_PILOT_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) was fixed after this inventory and before requesting any NIfTI header or voxel content. A separately frozen label-region experiment is required before full-case pixel decoding; no clinical or lesion-preservation result follows from a file-name join.
