# Tumor-region context audit: task-aware MRI prior-art boundary

This is a focused methods comparison based on the archived papers below, not a systematic review or a global priority claim. It was written while the frozen MSD BrainTumour 484-case CPU analysis was running. No interim cohort statistic was used to alter the endpoint.

| Work / reading level | What it actually tests | Consequence for the present claim |
|---|---|---|
| Fischer, Kuestner and Baumgartner 2023, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_fischer_mri_uq_fulltext_result.md) | PHiRec samples 20 reconstructions from one accelerated knee MRI observation and propagates uncertainty to segmentation. | Reconstruction disagreement and segmentation error/uncertainty are established precedents. This is not the same fixed-target, released-neighbor context switch, but rules out a generic task-uncertainty novelty claim. |
| Morshuis et al. 2024, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_segmentation_guided_mri_fulltext_result.md) | Segmentation-guided diffusion varies reconstructions under the same SKM-TEA knee k-space input and compares segmentation bounds with repeated sampling. | Deliberately diverse reconstruction pairs and segmentation uncertainty are established. Its outcome metrics are not comparable to the present retrospective paired-error fraction. |
| Morshuis et al. 2025, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_four_seed_forward_citation_result.md) | *Mind the Detail* evaluates pathology recall and mAP for semantically diverse fastMRI+ knee reconstructions under fixed measured k-space. | A positive `Delta O` cannot be called fewer missed lesions or diagnostic gain; those require a separately validated task experiment. |
| Li et al. 2026, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_targeted_recent_fulltext_result.md) | SliceBridge repairs T1 slice intervals from bilateral context and evaluates whole-brain segmentation volumes. Its matched uncoupled-flow comparator has the same context. | Through-plane context repair and task analysis are established. Improved structural consistency did not yield a significant volume-error difference against the matched flow comparator. |
| Verschuur et al. 2022, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_verschuur_neonatal_t2_fulltext_result.md) | Neonatal T2 slice interpolation is assessed against tissue segmentation and volume bias. | T2 interpolation with a downstream segmentation check is not new. Motion and through-plane provenance matter. |
| Mosquera Rojas et al. 2026, [full-text audit](literature/2026-10-01_targeted_recent_fulltext_result.md) | Glioma diagnosis/segmentation UQ measures case error detection, calibration and selective prediction against task labels. | A retrospective target-dependent `O` is not a deployable lesion-risk or calibrated error score. |
| Kuestner et al. 2024, [access audit](literature/2026-10-01_kuestner_fulltext_access_audit.md) | Formal abstract addresses deep-ensemble MRI reconstruction uncertainty; methods remain unread. | Exact intervention-level non-overlap remains an open publication gate. |

The frozen [MSD protocol](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) has two joint primary tests: positive patient-equal `Delta O` inside registered-release tumor labels, and positive tumor-minus-same-slice local label-negative `Delta O`, each with a patient-bootstrap lower bound above zero and at least 350/484 complete cases. A positive result would support only a **processed-cohort, annotated-region context effect** for the fixed IXI HAT/SwinIR pair. The labels come from multi-modality BRaTS annotations, while predictions use only the T2 channel. They do not prove tumor visibility to a T2-only reader, lesion preservation, original independent acquisition identity or equal-budget reconstruction benefit.

The four-neighbor PCHIP comparator in the same protocol is essential: phase-2 inference has extra real planes relative to a deployable phase-0-only x4 input. Even if an ensemble beats its shared-context version, it must be compared with interpolation given those extra planes before performance language is considered. A negative tumor-minus-local result would directly reject the proposed region-specific claim, despite a positive global-support effect.
