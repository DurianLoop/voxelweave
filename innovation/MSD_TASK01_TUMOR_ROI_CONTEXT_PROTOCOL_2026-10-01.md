Use the existing IXI-trained HAT (`2fae831ab933cf260972260ebec4349f3895106ac7b4d1af3ba368f407498eae`) and SwinIR (`6a819e47e65bd96c2d6e5b6d3adc212e1a46347080a0a661c602367be8a6f323`) checkpoints unchanged. Reuse the exact `prepared`, `predict` and `metrics` implementation in `experiments/remind_native_t2_ixi_pair.py`: observed-anchor min/max per phase, 256-square model input, resize back to native 240-square output. Analyze only the 3rd spatial axis of channel `3=T2w` from the registered 4D image, with no resampling, intensity relabeling or use of the FLAIR/T1/T1gd channels in predictions.

For depth 155, phase-0 anchors are `z=0,4,8,...` and phase-2 anchors `z=2,6,10,...`. Candidate targets are odd `z` with residue 1 or 3 modulo 4 and at least two actual anchors on **both** sides in **both** phases. The target layer is never an input. Define whole tumor from the paired label as codes 1, 2 or 3; any unexpected code fails the case. Before computing model outputs, keep candidate target layers with at least 64 whole-tumor label pixels. Within each residue's ascending eligible list, choose positions `floor((m-1)/3)` and `ceil(2(m-1)/3)`, distinct. A case requires two selected targets per residue; otherwise record a label/target qualification failure, with no substitute case or extra target. This yields four preselected tumor-positive target layers per complete case. Label values are used only for target/ROI selection and later evaluation, never supplied to the interpolators.

For each selected target, construct the original acquired-only foreground support from the two neighboring **phase-0 observed T2 planes**: a pixel is included if either anchor's absolute value exceeds 1% of its own maximum absolute value. The lesion ROI is label `1|2|3` intersected with this support; require at least 64 pixels. To form a local same-area control, take exactly that many supported label-0 pixels nearest the lesion by 2D Euclidean distance; break equal-distance ties by row-major pixel index. If insufficient normal pixels exist, the target and case fail. This control is label-negative *on the same target plane*, not a radiologist-certified healthy tissue sample. Report lesion, local-control and full-support areas for every target; do not choose ROIs by prediction error or visual appearance.

The fixed HAT phase-0 prediction is `a`; SwinIR phase-0/phase-2 predictions are `b0`/`b2`; withheld T2 channel target is `y`. Evaluate all three on exactly the same target and support. In each ROI separately, `T=(MSE(a,y)+MSE(b,y))/2`, `O=mean(((a-b)/2)^2)/T`, and `Delta O=O(a,b2)-O(a,b0)`. Reject zero/nonfinite denominator, missing/constant anchors or nonfinite pixels without substitution. First average the two targets within each residue, then residues equally within a complete case, then cases equally. No model/threshold tuning, favorable-label subclass, alternate orientation or post-result case filter is allowed.

## Primary decision and controls

The two jointly required primary estimates are the patient-equal `Delta O` **inside tumor** and the patient-equal contrast `Delta O_tumor - Delta O_local_control`. For each, use a paired 5,000-draw patient bootstrap with seed `20261005` and report the 2.5/97.5 percentiles. Declare a *tumor-region-specific context effect* only if at least **350/484** cases complete all four targets and **both** lower 95% bounds are strictly above zero. Otherwise report nonconfirmation, including all failures, signs, the exact complete-case denominator and both intervals; do not rescue the claim with a subgroup or a different ROI. The same patient bootstrap is descriptive conditional on this later-informed source/model choice and does not correct research selection.

Always report global-support `Delta O`, lesion and local-normal shared/disjoint `O`, both companions' lesion MSE/MAE, equal-weight shared/disjoint ensemble lesion MSE, and raw output-disagreement MSE. Recompute `O_disjoint` with the **shared-condition ROI-specific paired MSE** as a fixed denominator to test the arithmetic denominator explanation. As a same-available-observation performance comparator, interpolate each withheld target from the four real surrounding even planes `z-3,z-1,z+1,z+3` using PCHIP; compare its lesion and local-normal MSE with the disjoint HAT/SwinIR average. This PCHIP analysis is descriptive and does not replace the two primary tests. Phase-2 planes are extra observations relative to the original phase-0-only x4 input, so no deployable x4 improvement follows even if the ensemble beats PCHIP here. Report edema (1) and tumor-core (2|3) ROI results only as labeled secondary breakdowns, with their zero-count cases visible.
