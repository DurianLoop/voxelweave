# MSD BrainTumour tumor-region context audit: complete result

The [protocol](MSD_TASK01_TUMOR_ROI_CONTEXT_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) was frozen before MSD voxels or predictions were read. Its exact official-source [inventory](MSD_TASK01_OFFICIAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), [484/484 release-grid header gate](MSD_TASK01_ALL_HEADER_RESULT_2026-10-01.md), and [label qualification](experiments/msd_task01_label_qualification.json) preceded the CPU-only model run. The full [summary](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/summary.json), [per-patient table](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/per_patient.csv), [per-target table](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/per_target.csv), 484 [case records](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/cases), and [run contract](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/run_contract.json) are archived. The independent [arithmetic/denominator verifier](experiments/verify_msd_task01_tumor_roi.py) returned `verified: true` with 484 attempts, 483 complete patients and 1,932 complete targets. No GPU, running training job or locked IXI test image was used.

The fixed 484-case attempt denominator yielded **483 complete patients and 1,932 targets**. `BRATS_044` was the sole failure: the pre-inference label qualification recorded `URLError`, and it remained in the denominator without replacement. Both prespecified lower CI bounds exceed zero and the 350-case floor is met, so the protocol's **tumor-region-specific context-effect decision is positive**. The *incremental* tumor-versus-local contrast is small compared with the context effect shared by both regions, and its sign is positive in only 267/483 patients. This is a later-informed external task-region check after positive MIRA source results, not a blind discovery cohort; bootstrap intervals do not correct that research-selection history.

The [post-result descriptive check](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/posthoc_descriptive.json), produced by [this script](experiments/describe_msd_task01_posthoc.py), found a tumor-minus-local median of +0.5320 points, a 10%-trimmed mean of +0.5365 points, and a leave-one-patient-out mean range of +0.5504 to +0.6107 points. These diagnostics do not replace the two frozen endpoints or provide additional confirmatory tests. Holding each ROI's shared paired-MSE denominator fixed yields tumor `Delta O` +11.9683 points versus local +11.3121 points, a descriptive contrast of +0.6562 points. Thus a changed `O` denominator alone does not explain the observed mean regional contrast; anatomy, release processing and phase/context differences remain unresolved.

**No reconstruction-performance or diagnostic-benefit claim follows.** On the exact four surrounding real even planes available to the disjoint comparison, the prespecified PCHIP interpolation had patient-mean **tumor MSE 236.57** versus **1,208.05** for the equal-weight HAT0/SwinIR2 average. PCHIP was lower for **482/483** complete patients; the ratio of patient-mean MSEs was 5.11. In the equal-area local label-negative region, PCHIP was 1,608.37 versus ensemble 2,737.95, lower for 465/483 patients. The disjoint ensemble did improve on its shared-context counterpart inside tumor (1,208.05 versus 1,401.18 MSE), but this comparison gives the disjoint model extra observed planes and is decisively weaker than the equal-observation PCHIP control. The patient-mean relative percentage improvement against PCHIP is numerically unstable when individual PCHIP MSE is small; use the absolute means and paired win counts above instead.

The [summary](experiments/msd_task01_tumor_roi_context/summary.json) also reports both companions' ROI-specific MSE/MAE, shared/disjoint `O`, raw output-disagreement MSE, fixed-denominator sensitivity, PCHIP, and the edema/tumor-core breakdown. Edema had nonzero pixels on all 1,932 completed targets. Tumor core had nonzero pixels on 1,769/1,932 targets and at least one positive target in 482/483 patients; one patient had none. These are labeled secondary descriptions, without subgroup rescue of the primary decision. The independent verifier recomputed the formula, unchanged HAT reference error, four frozen targets, equal-area controls, patient aggregation, bootstrap, all failures and reported region counts/means from case files.

The only supported new statement is that, in this **registered BRaTS release**, the frozen IXI HAT/SwinIR published-neighbor context switch increases a retrospective paired-error visibility fraction slightly more inside the multi-modality annotated tumor mask than in a same-slice nearby label-negative control. The 1 mm release grid is not native acquisition slice spacing. These labels do not establish T2-only reader performance, and the anonymous MSD cases cannot currently be proven patient-disjoint from every other TCIA/BRaTS-derived source. `O` requires the withheld target truth and the phase-2 condition uses extra real planes; it is not a deployable risk score. The [task-aware prior-art matrix](MSD_TASK01_TASK_PRIOR_ART_MATRIX_2026-10-01.md) already rules out broad novelty around reconstruction diversity, segmentation uncertainty, lesion detection or through-plane task evaluation. Kuestner 2024 methods-level access, original independent-acquisition mechanism, IXI-derived weight redistribution rights, clean reproduction and formal author/venue review remain open gates.

**Result-after analytic falsification:** A separately frozen [PCHIP0/linear0-versus-linear2 control](MSD_TASK01_ANALYTIC_CONTEXT_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) on the same 483 patients yielded tumor-minus-local `Delta O=+0.9428` points (exploratory 95% patient CI +0.5503 to +1.3381), versus the neural pair's +0.5819 points. The paired difference interval crosses zero. This removes any basis for describing the regional contrast as a deep-network-specific phenomenon; the original frozen statistical decision itself is unchanged.
