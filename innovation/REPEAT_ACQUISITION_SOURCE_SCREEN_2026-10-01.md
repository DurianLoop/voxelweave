# Repeated-acquisition source screen: two official leads

This is a bounded, **metadata-only** source search on 2026-10-01. It does not establish an eligible pixel cohort or permission to access one. No candidate images were downloaded, no held-out target pixels were read and no training process was touched.

| Source | Repeat/contrast evidence | Access and task fit | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Zenodo 8234101](ZENODO_SCAN_RESCAN_SOURCE_GATE_2026-10-01.md), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.8234101` | Publisher description: five subjects x three 3T scanners x two acquisitions; each includes T2w SPACE and T2w FLAIR, along with quantitative maps. | API label `other-open` is superseded for our decision by the accompanying restrictive CWRU EULA: academic nonprofit use and prior written consent for copying/modification/distribution. Five subjects and scanner changes also limit a patient-level claim. | **Do not use without institutional/rightsholder clearance.** Only official record and license terms were reviewed. |
| [TCIA RIDER-NEURO-MRI](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/rider-neuro-mri/), DOI `10.7937/K9/TCIA.2015.VOSN3HN1` | Publisher description: 19 recurrent glioblastoma patients, repeated imaging ~2 days apart (one patient ~1 day); repeated DCE-MRI on same 1.5T magnet. T2-weighted images selected 16 DCE locations. 17 patients also had 3D post-contrast FLAIR, but the page does not by itself prove two matching native T2w series suitable for our fixed-target contrast. | TCIA page labels the image access **NIH Controlled Data Access Policy**. DataCite lists both controlled-access rights and CC BY 3.0; the latter does not grant access by itself. Public NBIA `getSeries` queries using `RIDER NEURO MRI` and `RIDER-NEURO-MRI` returned HTTP 200 with zero bytes, so no series-level T2 inventory was established. | **Do not use under current access.** If approved access is obtained, first conduct a header-only inventory and require matching T2 protocol, acquisition chronology, plane centers, processing and registration before freezing any target-pixel analysis. |

The TCIA collection page reports 19 subjects/108 studies/368 series and requires the Barboriak 2015 data citation. It also notes face-reconstruction risk from some images. The official [DataCite DOI record](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.7937%2FK9%2FTCIA.2015.VOSN3HN1) records the mixed rights statements. No public metadata response here proves which individual planes are original acquisitions; no identity-causal experiment is authorized by a scan-rescan title alone.

These leads do not alter the current [published-plane MIRA manuscript](PUBLIC_ONLY_MIRA_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md), whose two open sources are MSValid and ReMIND. The next source search should prioritize genuinely open, explicitly repeat-acquired T2 with accessible nonpixel acquisition metadata. Keep rights, header eligibility and same-target matching as gates before decoding any pixels.
