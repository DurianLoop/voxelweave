# Research priorities after the all-T2 header screen

This note integrates the archived paper readings with the current project evidence. It is a bounded literature assessment, not an exhaustive novelty search or a claim that any untested method is new. The archive currently contains 25 source PDFs and prior full-paper notes; this continuation rechecked the extracted passages for CRIS, SafeRestore and pixelwise MRI uncertainty against the earlier [interpolation](literature/2026-09-30_interpolation_prior_art.md), [diffusion](literature/2026-09-30_diffusion_prior_art.md), [focused five-paper](literature/2026-09-30_focused_additional_papers.md) and [uncertainty](literature/2026-09-30_mira_uncertainty_neighbours.md) reviews.

| Priority | Narrow question and nearest paper constraint | Evidence now / decisive next test |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Publishable measurement audit | Fixed MRI interpolation models, same target, shared versus disjoint *published* context. Hu 2021 already predicts MRI reconstruction error; Giannakopoulos 2026 already trains pixelwise uncertainty intervals. The novelty candidate is the explicit context-switch audit and paired error alignment, not error prediction or an uncertainty method. | IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR gives `Delta O=+24.1856` points on MSValid and `+26.3999` on ReMIND; a linear partner also gives +20.5445/+22.9386. Keep the linear control, phase/geometric confounding, ReMIND `MPR`, ranking negative result and patient-source selection history in the [public-only draft](PUBLIC_ONLY_MIRA_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md). Next work is citation/rights/author/venue review, not another algebraic score on the same predictions. |
| 2. Original-acquisition identity, conditional | A genuinely separate MRI measurement at the same centers could distinguish identity from phase/content/geometry. The local [manifest-linked 64-pair screen](FASTMRI_MANIFEST_LINKED_PAIR_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) and literal-label screen give the same four technical leads but zero timing-supported candidates and no release-rights proof. | Obtain acquisition/export records, exact official dataset terms and sequence classification. If independently repeated raw scans are documented, freeze eligibility, registration, anatomy/normalization controls and target-pixel analysis *before* opening pixels. Without this, do not use the word causal or raw-plane identity for MIRA. |
| 3. Forward-model ambiguity, conditional | HiFi-Diff already handles arbitrary offsets; CRIS already uses self-supervised orthogonal stripe completion and variable gaps; IPMI 2025/FOCVS and ECLARE already use acquisition operators or thickness/gap concepts. A narrow unresolved question is whether a held-out real observation can **falsify a wrong slice profile** and predict missing-slice harm beyond a fixed profile/ensemble. | On training-only data, first create a small known-profile synthetic factorial with matched center spacing and varied blur; compare held-out-observation prediction to actual missing-layer error at equal anchor count. Stop if the profile is unidentifiable or only the held-out residual improves. True thick-to-thin recovery needs a separate independently acquired thin reference. This remains a hypothesis, not a demonstrated contribution. |
| 4. Action selection / deployable safety, currently stopped | DRIFT already uses deterministic prediction plus residual flow and difficulty-aware computation. SafeRestore already selects restoration actions and certifies bounded risks on separate data. Adding a gate or uncertainty head alone is not novel. | Existing local patch/case selectors yield only tiny or structure-negative gains; the ReMIND target-ranking improvement interval crosses zero, and the disjoint score requires unavailable phase-2 slices. Do not train another gate or advertise calibrated deployment until a new source and acquired-only score with a supervised error-prediction baseline are preregistered. |

The strongest current claim is an observational, cross-source context audit. The present result cannot establish a pure acquisition-identity mechanism, a new reconstruction algorithm, clinical safety or a deployable risk score. These limits are scientific results, not missing prose. Publication readiness still depends on formal source citations, redistribution obligations, authorship, venue format and a reproducible release package; see the [claim gate](CLAIM_PUBLICATION_GATE_2026-09-30.md).

Later [full-text novelty refresh](literature/2026-10-01_shared_context_neighbours_fulltext.md): a 2026 measured-k-space-subspace posterior audit and a 2023 input-perturbation MRI error score are direct constraints on broad audit/scoring terminology, although neither tests this same-target published-slice context contrast. The official ReMIND data citation is now captured; the other publication gates remain.
