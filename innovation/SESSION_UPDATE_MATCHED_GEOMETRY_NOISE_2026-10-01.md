## Completed evidence

The IXI-trained fixed HAT/SwinIR pair previously gave `Delta O=+24.1856` percentage points on MSValid 84/84 patients and 479 targets, and `+26.3999` on ReMIND 62/63 patients and 248 targets. Common normalization and phase reversal retained the direction in both sources. A native-linear partner gave `+20.5445` and `+22.9386`, limiting any claim that the effect is specific to two learned architectures. ReMIND published DICOMs include `MPR`; the phase switch changes complete neighboring content and signed geometry.

The new [pre-inference plan](MSVALID_MATCHED_GEOMETRY_NOISE_CONTROL_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) asked whether independent versus shared **synthetic magnitude-noise realizations** matter when published anatomy, phase-0 slice geometry, target, HAT reference, normalization and support are matched. The [full result](MSVALID_MATCHED_GEOMETRY_NOISE_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) scored both 1% and 5% levels on the exact 479 targets for all 84 MSValid patients, with zero failures. Patient-weighted `Delta O_noise` was -0.000339 points (95% bootstrap interval -0.001348 to +0.000604; 41 positive/43 negative patients) at 1%, and +0.002529 points (+0.000276 to +0.004900; 46 positive/38 negative) at 5%. An independent read-only verifier passed exact target membership, patient/residue aggregation, fixed-seed intervals and all result totals. The 5% estimate is a small positive result, not an exact null.

The synthetic intervention did not reproduce the original +24.1856-point phase contrast. It does not identify a causal share for noise versus geometry or anatomical content, and cannot be extrapolated to true independent MRI acquisitions. The noise amplitudes are arbitrary, magnitude noise is approximate, the published plane supplies both clean signal and target, and the common normalization uses clean-anchor extrema. Current data do not satisfy the [original acquisition-identity source gate](ACQUISITION_ID_SOURCE_GATE_2026-10-01.md).
