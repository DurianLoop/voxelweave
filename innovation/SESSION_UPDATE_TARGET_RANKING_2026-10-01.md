# MRI innovation continuation: target-error ranking check

The active objective remains a defensible publishable claim for the MRI project. The user GPU training and locked test set were left untouched. This turn used only the already stored, hash-checked main and linear-control per-target CSVs. The prior context-audit claim has two positive source-specific means but is not a pure acquisition-identity effect: ReMIND DICOMs contain `MPR`, phase switching changes complete neighboring images and geometry, and a native-linear partner has a large phase contrast.

The new [pre-calculation plan](TWO_SOURCE_TARGET_ERROR_RANKING_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) asked a more practical falsification question: does increased disagreement identify the higher-error target **within the same patient and residue**? The [result](TWO_SOURCE_TARGET_ERROR_RANKING_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) covers all 84 MSValid patients/479 targets and 62 ReMIND patients/248 targets, with 651 and 124 within-residue target pairs. HAT/SwinIR shared-to-disjoint disagreement concordance rose from 0.6605 to 0.8017 in MSValid (patient-bootstrap difference +0.1411, 95% interval +0.0681 to +0.2161), but only 0.6129 to 0.6774 in ReMIND (+0.0645, interval -0.0242 to +0.1452). Both disjoint SwinIR scores ranked better than disjoint linear scores, but linear's phase change also had positive point estimates. An [independent verifier](experiments/verify_two_source_target_error_ranking.py) replayed joins, score reconstruction, patient/residue aggregation and both bootstrap procedures and passed.

**Decision:** the mean published-context error-observability contrast does not establish a reproducible target-level ranking improvement. ReMIND's interval includes zero; its 124 comparisons give only two pair decisions per patient. More fundamentally, phase-2 planes needed by the disjoint score are unavailable in the intended phase-0-only sparse acquisition. The [public-source manuscript](PUBLIC_ONLY_MIRA_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_2026-10-01.md), [claim gate](CLAIM_PUBLICATION_GATE_2026-09-30.md), [stress test](PUBLIC_ONLY_CLAIM_REVIEW_STRESS_TEST_2026-10-01.md), [queue](EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md) and [README](README.md) now retain this unfavorable result and prohibit a deployable-score claim.

Next work that can genuinely raise publication strength remains a separately sourced, predeclared original-plane or same-geometry acquisition design, plus venue-fit and current citation/release checks. Another post-result algebraic variant on these same two cohorts is unlikely to resolve the main causality and novelty objections. Current source/release gaps are tracked in the [citation audit](PUBLIC_SOURCE_RELEASE_CITATION_AUDIT_2026-10-01.md). The updated manuscript PDF hash and final archive verification are recorded below after rebuild. Research status remains active.

The [local fastMRI repeat-T2 header gate](FASTMRI_REPEAT_T2_HEADER_GATE_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) then screened all seven `REPEAT`-labeled candidate cases under a [frozen nonpixel rule](FASTMRI_REPEAT_T2_HEADER_GATE_PLAN_2026-10-01.md). It read 272 DICOM headers across 17 series: four within-case pairs were both T2-labeled and had matched centers; two lacked usable time evidence but otherwise met checked geometry/protocol fields, and two had matrix or TR mismatches as well. **Zero** passed the complete header gate, and the exact local release/access agreement is unverified. No patient pixels were read and no case IDs were written to output. This narrows a possible trace-back request to two anonymous candidate pairs without turning the local data into manuscript evidence.
