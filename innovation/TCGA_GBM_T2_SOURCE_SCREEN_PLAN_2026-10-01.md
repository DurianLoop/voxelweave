# TCGA-GBM as a possible independent public brain-T2 source: frozen metadata screen

**Frozen before retrieving the TCGA-GBM official collection page or NBIA series inventory.** This is a new-patient-source qualification for the fixed IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR published-slice-context audit. It does not test model performance or original acquisition identity and does not use GPU or locked IXI test images. TCGA-GBM must not be pooled with UPENN-GBM, MSValid or ReMIND without checking patient/source overlap and acquisition history.

Archive the official TCIA collection page and the exact `getSeries?Collection=TCGA-GBM&Modality=MR` response with URL, retrieval time, byte count and SHA-256. Require an official DOI/citation and usable research/publication license for the exact series; do not infer rights from other TCIA collections. Count all series, distinct patients, descriptions and license values. Candidate descriptions must contain literal `T2`, `T2W`, `TSE` or `TURBO SPIN ECHO`; exclude FLAIR, diffusion/ADC, susceptibility, T1, segmentation/map and postcontrast labels; require at least 24 images. Rank each literal description by distinct patient count, then series count, then lexical name. Do not combine descriptions or choose by image appearance/outcome.

Continue to a separately frozen, single-series **header-only** pilot only if a single description has at least 30 distinct patients, the official rights are clear, and selection of one baseline/pre-treatment series per patient is plausibly documented. The pilot must preselect the lexicographically first patient/series, read with `stop_before_pixels=True`, and verify `ORIGINAL/PRIMARY` provenance, true physical positions, 2D mode and processing history. `DERIVED`, `MPR`, resampling, ambiguous visit timing or absent provenance bars an original-plane claim. Any image-pixel or model experiment requires another protocol frozen before decoding targets, including IDs, plane rules, hashes, endpoint, patient-level statistic, failure handling and overlap audit. A processed-source study, if valuable, is a different question and must be labelled accordingly.
