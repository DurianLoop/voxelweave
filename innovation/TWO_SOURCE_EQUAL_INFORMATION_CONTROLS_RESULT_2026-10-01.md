# Equal-information interpolation controls for disjoint ensembling

**Decision:** the post-result disjoint HAT0/SwinIR2 average has a reproducible error reduction relative to the shared-context average, but it does **not** establish a cross-source robust advantage over standard interpolation given the same phase-0 plus phase-2 published planes. The [algebra plan](TWO_SOURCE_ENSEMBLE_ERROR_PLAN_2026-10-01.md), [linear plan](UNION_EVEN_LINEAR_CONTROL_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) and [cubic plan](UNION_EVEN_CUBIC_CONTROL_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) were each written before their respective calculations, but all are **post-main-result exploratory** analyses. They reuse the same 84 MSValid patients/479 targets and 62 ReMIND patients/248 targets; they are not three confirmations.

The first calculation obtained equal-weight ensemble MSE exactly from original saved target records, `E=T(1-O)`. Switching the companion from shared to disjoint published planes reduced this MSE for **479/479 MSValid and 248/248 ReMIND targets**. Patient-mean relative reductions were 24.58% (95% exploratory patient-bootstrap interval 23.74% to 25.42%) and 26.78% (25.93% to 27.66%), respectively. This is a consequence of the already reported large `Delta O` and near-stable `T`, not a new independent model run. Crucially, the disjoint partner observes phase-2 planes absent from the original phase-0-only x4 input.

To account for those added observations, the next two controls used the **same union of real even-index planes** available to HAT0 plus SwinIR2. Every original odd-index target and phase-0 foreground support was retained. Native linear used its real neighbors `z-1,z+1`; Catmull-Rom and PCHIP used real `z-3,z-1,z+1,z+3`. The table reports patient-mean relative gain of the learned disjoint ensemble over each direct interpolation baseline; positive favors the ensemble. Patient means first balance target residues 1 and 3, then patients. ReMIND intervals stratify 35 ordinary-T2 and 27 BLADE patients.

| Same-available-observation comparator | MSValid gain, 95% CI; patients better | ReMIND gain, 95% CI; patients better |
|---|---:|---:|
| Nearest two-plane linear | +7.61% [6.47%, 8.89%]; 84/84 | +2.41% [1.18%, 3.69%]; 38/62 |
| Four-plane Catmull-Rom | +11.71% [10.59%, 12.92%]; 84/84 | +4.19% [2.70%, 5.73%]; 43/62 |
| Four-plane shape-preserving PCHIP | +8.12% [6.94%, 9.33%]; 84/84 | **+0.91% [-0.52%, 2.42%]; 34/62** |

ReMIND PCHIP is the limiting comparison: 28/62 patients and 114/248 targets favor PCHIP, and its exploratory interval crosses zero. Under the [prewritten stopping rule](UNION_EVEN_CUBIC_CONTROL_PLAN_2026-10-01.md), **stop the cross-source actionable ensembling claim**. The two sources, models, targets and baselines were already selected through a long research history; none of these intervals adjusts for that selection, and four-point PCHIP is not a fully trained x2 model baseline. The positive MSValid result and weaker ReMIND contrast do not justify a new algorithm or a clinical performance claim. The original same-target `Delta O` published-context audit remains intact and distinct.

Source NIfTI/ZIP SHA-256, source qualification, the original 727 target identities and phase-0 support counts were checked in the CPU-only [linear](experiments/union_even_linear_control.py) and [cubic](experiments/union_even_cubic_control.py) runs. The cubic run also replayed every preceding linear MSE before calculating a new baseline. No GPU, checkpoint inference or locked IXI test data was used. Full [ensemble](experiments/two_source_ensemble_error/summary.json), [linear](experiments/union_even_linear_control/summary.json) and [cubic](experiments/union_even_cubic_control/summary.json) summaries link to per-target/per-patient CSVs. The [independent read-only verifier](experiments/verify_union_even_controls.py) checked all 727 target algebra records, all 146 patient aggregations and all four cubic-bootstrap intervals and returned `PASS`. Patient-linked CSVs stay internal.

The publication decision is therefore narrower: retain the retrospective context-dependence claim, disclose the equal-information PCHIP failure in any full results/supplement, and seek a fresh cohort plus an equal-input trained x2 comparator before reviving a practical ensembling method claim. The current five-page PDF predates this post-result control and should be rebuilt only when the manuscript is revised for a selected venue.
