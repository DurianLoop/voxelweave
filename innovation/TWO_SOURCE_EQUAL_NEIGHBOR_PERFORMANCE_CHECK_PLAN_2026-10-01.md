# Post-result, archived-CSV check of two-neighbor performance in MSValid and ReMIND

This exploratory check was written after the 483-case MSD tumor and preprocessing results, before calculating the following two-source joins. It asks whether the fixed IXI HAT/SwinIR pair also loses to raw two-neighbor phase-0 linear interpolation on the older **whole acquired-foreground support** cohorts. It is neither a new source nor a tumor-ROI replication.

For MSValid, join exactly 479 target rows from `msvalid_ixi_weights_secondary/per_target.csv` with `public_linear_partner_control/msvalid/per_target.csv`; require 84 patients. For ReMIND, join exactly 248 rows from `remind_native_t2_ixi_pair/per_target.csv` with `public_linear_partner_control/remind/per_target.csv`; require 62 completed patients. Join by `(patient,z,residue)`, require unique keys, identical support-pixel counts and z-spacing, and matching HAT reference MAE within `1e-5`. The model `shared_a_mse` is HAT0, `shared_b_mse` is SwinIR0, and the linear control `shared_b_mse` is raw linear0. Do not compare any phase-2/disjoint value or the `O` fractions here.

Average target MSE within each residue, residues equally within patient, then patients equally within each source. Report all three MSEs, HAT-minus-linear and SwinIR-minus-linear means and positive/negative/tied patient counts separately by source. Original intensity scales differ across sources, so never pool raw MSEs or infer a cohort-effect difference from their magnitude. No case/target selection, denominator change, image access, model inference or GPU use. These sources lack the MSD tumor labels and cannot establish a pathology-specific pattern. The older controls are result-after analyses, and this cross-read is further result-informed.
