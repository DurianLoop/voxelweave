# UPENN fixed-denominator post-result sensitivity: frozen calculation

Written after the positive UPENN result and before this calculation. Use only the frozen `experiments/upenn_gbm_processed_t2_context/per_target.csv`, `per_patient.csv` and `summary.json`; read no DICOM pixels or checkpoints. Refuse to overwrite outputs. Require 374 patients, four targets per patient, two residues with two targets each, finite positive paired MSEs and exact reproduction of the original patient-equal mean `Delta O` to numerical tolerance.

For every target retain the shared-condition paired MSE `T_s` in both denominators. With the archived shared/disjoint disagreement MSEs `D_s,D_d`, calculate `Q_s=D_s/(4T_s)` and `Q_d=D_d/(4T_s)` and `Delta Q=Q_d-Q_s`. Verify `Q_s` equals the stored shared `O`, and `D_d/(4T_d)` equals stored disjoint `O`. Average the two targets within each residue, then both residues equally within each patient; patients receive equal weight. Report both `Q` means, `Delta Q`, patient/target sign counts and a 5,000-draw patient bootstrap percentile interval with fixed seed 20261001. Also report original `Delta O` for comparison. These are descriptive post-result intervals, not new independent confirmation or causal decomposition.

Interpretation stop rule: a positive fixed-denominator contrast rules out the changing denominator as the sole arithmetic source of the original `Delta O`. It does not prove original acquisition identity, clinical gain or deployability because target truth remains in `T_s` and the disjoint context changes full neighboring images and geometry.
