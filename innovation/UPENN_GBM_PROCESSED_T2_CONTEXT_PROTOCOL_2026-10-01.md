# Frozen processed-UPENN-GBM published-plane context audit

**Frozen before any UPENN-GBM pixel decoding or model prediction.** The official [metadata inventory](literature/upenn_gbm_t2_metadata_screen.json) and one [header-only pilot](UPENN_GBM_T2_SOURCE_AND_HEADER_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) were already examined. That pilot found `DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER` in 64/64 headers and stopped the **original-acquisition identity** branch. This new study asks a different question: does the existing fixed IXI HAT/SwinIR pair retain its same-target **released-slice-context** effect on an independent, explicitly processed TCIA series? Its answer cannot establish independent native acquired planes, pre-treatment status, true thin-slice recovery or clinical safety. No GPU or locked IXI test images are used.

## Source and selection

Use only the archived official NBIA `getSeries?Collection=UPENN-GBM&Modality=MR` response, SHA-256 `bbae9a76f75a42cc35d460448c3afa87c55119d4ee5a84b9b18179fe28908de9`, and TCIA collection DOI `10.7937/TCIA.709X-DN49`, CC BY 4.0. Select the exact literal description `Axial T2 tse: Processed_CaPTk`, at least 24 images. For every distinct `PatientID`, choose one row by `(StudyDate,SeriesInstanceUID)` ascending; sort selected patients lexically. The frozen inventory contains 402 candidate series and **374** selected patients. All 374 enter the attempt, including the previously header-screened first patient; no study-description or appearance-based subset, replacement patient, alternate sequence or result-driven stopping is allowed. The earliest available study is **not** assumed pre-treatment. Anonymous IDs cannot prove absolute non-overlap with other collections.

Download each selected series only from the official NBIA `getImage?SeriesInstanceUID=...` endpoint under git-ignored `innovation/data/upenn_gbm_processed_t2_audit/`, reusing only the already verified first-series ZIP if its UID/hash match. Record complete ZIP SHA-256, bytes and member count; retry failed transfers for the **same UID** only. Preserve all failures. Do not include raw DICOM or patient images in a publication package.

## Nonpixel qualification before targets

For every ZIP read every `.dcm` with `pydicom.dcmread(..., stop_before_pixels=True)`. Require ZIP CRC, member count matching official `ImageCount`, one matching PatientID/StudyUID/SeriesUID, unique SOP UIDs, the selected series description, 2D acquisition mode, single-channel `MONOCHROME2`, consistent Rows/Columns, PixelSpacing within 0.5%, ImageOrientationPatient within `1e-4`, at least 24 unique physical positions and adjacent projected physical steps within 2% of their median. Sort by projection on the slice normal, never filename or InstanceNumber. Record all ImageType values, processing fields, nominal thickness/spacing and actual physical step; **derived** provenance is expected and reported, not an eligibility failure. Do not rely on `SpacingBetweenSlices` for ordering, especially where it conflicts with physical positions. Any malformed header or missing ZIP is a qualification failure; retain the patient in the denominator and do not replace it.

For each eligible depth `n`, phase-0 anchors are `0,4,8,...`, phase-2 anchors `2,6,10,...`. Candidate targets are indices with `z mod 4` equal to 1 or 3 and at least two real anchors on **each side in both phases**. Within each residue's ascending candidate list of length `m`, choose positions `floor((m-1)/3)` and `ceil(2(m-1)/3)` (distinct). If either residue lacks two candidates, qualification fails. Freeze these four target indices from headers alone before any target pixel is decoded.

## Fixed model and endpoint

Use IXI-trained HAT `baseline/ixi_hat_2p5d/runs/full_test115/checkpoints/hat_epoch_best.pth`, SHA-256 `2fae831ab933cf260972260ebec4349f3895106ac7b4d1af3ba368f407498eae`, and IXI-trained SwinIR `baseline/ixi_swinir_2p5d/runs/full/checkpoints/swinir_epoch_best.pth`, SHA-256 `6a819e47e65bd96c2d6e5b6d3adc212e1a46347080a0a661c602367be8a6f323`. CPU float32, at most two Torch threads, no AMP or GPU. Reuse the exact `prepared`, `predict`, `metrics` and patient-equal/residue-equal scoring contract in `experiments/remind_native_t2_ixi_pair.py`: decode only all even anchors plus the four frozen odd targets; apply DICOM slope/intercept; observed-only min/max per phase; resize to 256 square for model input and back to native shape; fixed HAT phase-0 reference `a`, SwinIR phase-0 companion `b0`, SwinIR phase-2 companion `b2`, same target `y`; support from the two phase-0 neighboring acquired planes only. Reject nonfinite/constant data, empty support, invalid denominator or inference failure without substitution.

Per target, `T=(MSE(a,y)+MSE(b,y))/2`, `O=mean(((a-b)/2)^2)/T`, `Delta O=O_disjoint-O_shared`. First average two targets within each residue, then the two residues equally within each complete patient, then complete patients equally. Main point estimate is patient-mean `Delta O`; 95% percentile CI uses 5,000 patient bootstrap draws with seed `20261004`. Report all 374 attempted, download/header/pixel failures, complete patients, targets, positive/negative patients, both `O` values, paired and individual MAE/MSE, both residues, physical spacing and ImageType distribution. No result-based model/threshold change. Primary success requires at least **300/374 complete patients** and the patient-bootstrap lower 95% bound strictly above zero; otherwise report nonconfirmation. Any study-description or geometry subgroup is descriptive only, with no substituted success criterion.

An effect here would strengthen a **processed published-plane context audit** and test the current claim under a distinct release pipeline; it would not repair the missing original-acquisition counterfactual. An absent/reversed effect would narrow the cross-pipeline claim. ReMIND/MSValid outcomes and earlier controls were known before this protocol, so this is a later, informed external check rather than a pristine discovery cohort. Keep the previously selected HAT/SwinIR pair and all failures visible.
