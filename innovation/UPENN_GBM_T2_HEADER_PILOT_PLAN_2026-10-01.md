**Frozen after official series metadata review, before downloading the selected DICOM ZIP or reading any pixels.** The [metadata screen](UPENN_GBM_T2_SOURCE_SCREEN_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) archived the official TCIA collection page, SHA-256 `12dfaa491640338ab7ba553776bf95aef29f9a349a725c0535af9ebfa9cb24c2`, and 3,680 MR series from official NBIA `getSeries`, SHA-256 `bbae9a76f75a42cc35d460448c3afa87c55119d4ee5a84b9b18179fe28908de9`. Every series advertises CC BY 4.0 and collection DOI `10.7937/TCIA.709X-DN49`. The most frequent literal axial T2 description meeting 24 images is `Axial T2 tse: Processed_CaPTk`: 402 series for 374 patients. The official page states that released NIfTI volumes were skull-stripped and atlas-registered and do not align with the DICOM images; the DICOM series name itself still warrants caution. `StudyDesc=BRAIN^ROUTINE` does not establish pre-treatment timing.

Select exactly the lexicographically first `PatientID`, then `(StudyDate,SeriesInstanceUID)`, among those 402 series. In the frozen inventory this is `UPENN-GBM-00020`, series UID `1.3.6.1.4.1.14519.5.2.1.304664131632340855252559437288625814808`, 64 images. Download only its official NBIA `getImage?SeriesInstanceUID=...` ZIP under the git-ignored `innovation/data/upenn_gbm_t2_header_pilot/`, recording URL, ZIP byte size and SHA-256. Verify member count and ZIP integrity. Use `pydicom.dcmread(..., stop_before_pixels=True)` for all DICOMs and check that PixelData was not loaded.

Record technical header values and missingness for study/series identity, StudyDate, AcquisitionDate/Time, `ImageType`, `MRAcquisitionType`, SourceImageSequence and derivation tags, matrix, PixelSpacing, orientation, ImagePositionPatient, slice thickness/spacing, TR/TE, unique SOP UID and physical plane order. Do not record private tags, names, births or image pixels in the public-facing result. Compare all headers for within-series consistency. A `DERIVED`, `MPR`, resampled or missing provenance indicator bars any **original acquired-plane** interpretation. If the series is clearly processed/registered, stop the native 2D T2 expansion. If the header alone is ambiguous, seek official processing documentation; do not use pixels to decide whether the source is admissible. Even an original/primary 2D result would only justify a separately frozen full-cohort and target-pixel protocol, not an immediate MIRA confirmation.
