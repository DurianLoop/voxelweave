# UPENN-GBM official metadata screen and frozen T2 header pilot

This is a **source-qualification result**, not a model test. The [metadata rule](UPENN_GBM_T2_SOURCE_SCREEN_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) was fixed before retrieving the official inventory; the [single-series rule](UPENN_GBM_T2_HEADER_PILOT_PLAN_2026-10-01.md) was fixed before downloading its ZIP. No target pixels, GPU inference, or locked IXI test images were used.

| Stage | Observed evidence | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Official collection | TCIA [UPENN-GBM collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/upenn-gbm/) and [NBIA `getSeries`](https://services.cancerimagingarchive.net/nbia-api/services/v1/getSeries?Collection=UPENN-GBM&Modality=MR), retrieved 2026-10-01 00:31:41 UTC. Archived page SHA-256 `12dfaa491640338ab7ba553776bf95aef29f9a349a725c0535af9ebfa9cb24c2`; inventory SHA-256 `bbae9a76f75a42cc35d460448c3afa87c55119d4ee5a84b9b18179fe28908de9`. [Structured screen](literature/upenn_gbm_t2_metadata_screen.json). | 3,680 MR series from 630 patients. Every inventory row reports CC BY 4.0 and DOI `10.7937/TCIA.709X-DN49`. Rights and inventory size justify a technical pilot, not an image-based experiment. |
| Selected stratum | The most frequent literal axial T2 description with at least 24 images was `Axial T2 tse: Processed_CaPTk`: 402 series from 374 patients. The lexicographically first patient/series was frozen before image retrieval. | The description already signals processing; `StudyDesc=BRAIN^ROUTINE` cannot establish pre-treatment status. The official page describes skull stripping and atlas registration for released NIfTI volumes and warns those do not align with DICOM images. It does not prove the DICOM series is raw. |
| Frozen first series | One official NBIA ZIP, 3,008,320 bytes, SHA-256 `54cfde800818f965da8378da66e9d3019b9a865bf335e50d67f7c53a35883f70`. ZIP integrity, 64 DICOM members, matching study/series identifiers and 64 distinct SOP UIDs were verified. [Machine-readable result](literature/upenn_gbm_t2_header_pilot.json); [independent read-only verifier](experiments/verify_upenn_gbm_t2_header_pilot.py). | All 64 headers say `ImageType=DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER`; all also say `MRAcquisitionType=2D`. The latter does not override the derived/secondary provenance. `SourceImageSequence` and `DerivationCodeSequence` are absent, so the exact transformation cannot be reconstructed from these headers. |
| Geometry | Matrix 256 x 208, pixel spacing 0.9375 x 0.9375 mm, 64 unique projected physical positions. Consecutive projected positions are 2.999999--3.000001 mm apart. `SliceThickness=3 mm`, but `SpacingBetweenSlices=1 mm` in every header. | Header spacing and actual physical positions disagree; physical positions must govern ordering. No pixel-based claim follows. |

The independent verifier returned `PASS`: all 64 headers were re-read with `stop_before_pixels=True`; ZIP hash, membership, identity, derived flags and projected spacing agreed with the frozen result. Its output reports `pixel_data_read=false`. The raw ZIP stays under the git-ignored `innovation/data/upenn_gbm_t2_header_pilot/` path.

**Decision:** stop expansion of this selected `Processed_CaPTk` stratum as an *original independently acquired 2D T2* source. Do not download the remaining 401 series or decode targets under the present plan. This first-series finding does **not** establish that every one of the 402 series is derived, nor that all UPENN-GBM T2 material is unusable for every research question. A processed-volume domain-shift study would need a separately defined task, timing/provenance documentation and a new pre-pixel protocol; it cannot close the present original-acquisition identity gate. The existing IXI/MSValid/ReMIND published-slice-context claim is unchanged.
