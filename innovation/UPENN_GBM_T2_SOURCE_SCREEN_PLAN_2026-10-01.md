# UPENN-GBM as a possible third public brain-T2 source: metadata-only screen

**Frozen before retrieving UPENN-GBM series inventory or image files.** Purpose: determine whether this official TCIA collection can support a genuinely new-patient-source, public-rights brain T2 external test of the existing fixed IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR published-slice-context audit. This is a source gate, not a new primary experiment, not an original-acquisition identity claim and not a request to use GPU or the locked IXI test set.

First archive the official TCIA collection webpage (or structured collection metadata) and the exact NBIA `getSeries?Collection=UPENN-GBM&Modality=MR` response, with URL, retrieval time and SHA-256. Check the collection DOI, version, data license and any accompanying terms. If clear public research use and publication attribution cannot be established from official materials, stop before image download. Do not infer rights from a secondary mirror.

From the complete series inventory, count patients, MR series and all distinct descriptions. Candidate T2 series must have a literal T2/T2W/TSE/TURBO-SPIN-ECHO description, must not be FLAIR, DWI/ADC, susceptibility, T1, segmentation, derived map or contrast-enhanced T2, and must have at least 24 images. Prefer a single common pre-treatment axial 2D T2 sequence description with one eligible series per patient. Do not mix `T2` and `T2 FLAIR`, pre-/post-treatment visits, or multiple descriptions to increase sample size. If acquisition phase is not discernible from official series/study metadata, record the ambiguity and require DICOM headers before freezing a cohort. Do not choose patients or sequence names based on image quality or the MIRA endpoint.

Advance only if the official source has a usable license, at least 30 distinct candidate patients in one sequence/protocol stratum, and a plausible unambiguous pre-treatment series selection. Then write a separate fixed header-only pilot on the lexicographically first eligible patient/series, read DICOM public technical tags with `stop_before_pixels=True`, and require coherent physical ordering, original/primary 2D provenance, matrix, pixel spacing, orientation and slice-count support. Any positive source qualification would require a separately frozen full-cohort protocol, patient IDs, target planes, model hashes, statistic, failure handling and source hashes **before** decoding target pixels. This new source would be a later test informed by MSValid/ReMIND, not a blind discovery or proof of pure acquisition identity.
