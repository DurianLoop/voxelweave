# Fixed training-case full-volume software preflight

This is written after authenticating the original-contour conversion on 001/182 and the MONAI list-to-tuple compatibility on 001, before full-volume reconstruction task inference. Patient 001 is a known task-model training patient with prior four-target reconstruction results. Its results are development evidence only. No held-out task patients are scored in this preflight, and no method/threshold may be selected by its Dice.

Use native VS-SEG-001 T2, the independently checked original JSON+official SlicerRT mask from conversion attempt 03, and the authenticated published T2 segmenter. The observed information is exactly native planes 0,4,...,76. All methods preserve those planes bit for bit, reconstruct all 57 internal missing planes (remainders 1/2/3), and copy observed plane 76 into the three unsupported terminal planes 77/78/79. Record this edge convention; those terminal planes do not count as image-recovery targets. Reference native voxels and lesion labels never enter reconstruction, normalization, model inputs, or the target choice.

Conditions are native, raw two-neighbor linear, raw local four-anchor PCHIP, pipeline linear, pipeline local four-anchor PCHIP, frozen IXI HAT and frozen IXI SwinIR. PCHIP uses anchors i-1,i,i+1,i+2 when all exist and falls back to the same adjacent linear rule for the two end intervals. Pipeline methods use the unchanged observed-only normalization and 256x256 antialiasing resize in prepared/predict; their generated normalized planes are clipped to [0,1], resized back and denormalized. Raw PCHIP is unclipped. Existing HAT/SwinIR checkpoint hashes remain fixed.

Image checks cover the union of >1%-maximum-absolute-value foreground masks of the two adjacent observed anchors, with identical masks for all methods. Record missing-plane normalized MSE in full support and original lesion intersected with that support, both target-equal and pixel-weighted summaries; empty lesion slices contribute zero pixels and are not mislabeled as zero error. Also store unrestricted full-image MSE, all per-plane errors and reconstructed volume hashes.

For the task preflight, convert each full reconstruction to RAS by the independently checked native orientation, apply the original MONAI full-volume NormalizeIntensity, and run the tuple-equivalent Gaussian sliding window (384x384x64; batch 1; unchanged overlap/sigma). Score the full native-grid original label with Dice, TP/FP/FN and signed/absolute volume difference. Save reconstruction volumes and predictions for this one case. Do not censor poor native Dice, small/short tumors, or empty model predictions. The purpose is to verify all seven condition pipelines, grid preservation and bounded CPU cost; none of these training-case scores is an independent confirmation or a clinical-harm estimate.

One CPU worker, one Torch thread, process-local denial of CUDA initialization, below-normal priority for this process only, >=15 GB workspace free and >=8 GB available physical memory. Do not inspect locked IXI test or control any existing training process. Stop on technical/resource failure and retain the partial attempt and logs.
