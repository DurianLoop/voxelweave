# VS-SEG-001 T2/TV pixel and mask feasibility pilot

Written after the nonpixel TCIA source/contour gates passed, before decoding any VS-SEG T2 pixel array. This pilot checks whether native 3D T2 and the public T2 RTSTRUCT can yield a reproducible x4 lesion ROI. It is not a model evaluation or clinical outcome.

- Use only the exact two SHA-256-verified `VS-SEG-001` ZIPs in `innovation/data/vs_seg_001_header_pilot`. Sort the 80 T2 DICOM instances by physical position along the common slice normal, validate 1.5 mm spacing, and decode the unmodified `MONOCHROME2` pixel arrays as float32. No interpolation/resampling to a new image grid.
- For ROI `TV`, use the T2 RTSTRUCT's `CLOSED_PLANAR` contour points and their referenced SOPs. Transform patient coordinates to row/column pixel-center coordinates using DICOM ImagePositionPatient, ImageOrientationPatient and PixelSpacing; fill each polygon with `skimage.draw.polygon` on the referenced T2 slice. Reject contours outside the image or further than 0.001 mm from their referenced plane. Require one contour per tumor-positive slice and record all tumor areas. The label is RTSTRUCT-derived and may inherit T1-to-T2 contour interpolation; do not call it an independently annotated T2-only truth.
- Use phase-0 anchors at `z=0,4,8,...`. For each residue 1 and 3, identify withheld T2 slices with at least 64 TV pixels within acquired-anchor support. Support is the original ds007045 rule: a pixel is included if either adjacent phase-0 anchor has absolute value above 1% of that anchor's maximum absolute value. From each residue's sorted eligible list, choose two indices at floor((n-1)/3) and floor(2(n-1)/3); fail if fewer than two distinct choices. This target choice uses only the label/support, not reconstruction outcome.
- Compute raw two-anchor linear and raw four-phase-0-anchor PCHIP at the four targets, normalized MSE by squared observed-anchor max-minus-min and raw MAE in TV and support. Archive per-target results, source/protocol hashes, all failures and one tumor-slice overlay for visual QA. No model predictions, GPU, locked IXI test, or modification to the user's running training. A successful pilot only authorizes a separately frozen multi-patient model protocol after full source/label qualification; it does not prove a publishable claim.
