# VS-SEG T2 RTSTRUCT label-semantics audit after stopped exact-TV run

The original [frozen model protocol](VS_SEG_THIN3D_MODEL_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md) required an exact `TV` ROI. Its first 14 attempted cases produced only one complete case: six RTSTRUCTs lacked/ambiguously named `TV`, six had too few fixed x4 lesion targets, and one failed physical spacing. The batch was explicitly interrupted after case 14 **before** a cohort summary; no missing cases were counted as completed and no primary claim was evaluated. The output and downloads are retained. This source-level label mismatch justifies a new nonpixel audit, not reinterpreting that run as a success.

Before any revised model inference, download or reuse only the official T2 RTSTRUCT series for all 242 mapped patients. Record exact source ZIP hashes, every ROIName, ROIObservationLabel/type if present, contour counts, and patient/series UID consistency. Do not decode MR pixels or examine reconstruction outcomes. Compare observed names with the TCIA page, source paper, and the released conversion script's tumor-label selection. If the mapping from names to vestibular schwannoma is ambiguous or varies without a verifiable official rule, do not silently pick the largest ROI. A revised protocol must give an exact allowed-name mapping and keep unknown cases as failures, with the first stopped batch disclosed as development history.
