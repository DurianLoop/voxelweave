# Frozen VS-SEG native-3D T2 cross-pathology model protocol

Written after full-text, official metadata and `VS-SEG-001` **analytic-only** label feasibility checks, before any VS-SEG HAT/SwinIR inference. The prior ds007045 HAT lesion result motivated this question, so this is a prospective cross-pathology stress test of an existing signal, not a blind discovery. VS-SEG consists of 3D CISS/TSE T2 imaging of vestibular schwannoma and differs from glioma spin-echo T2; do not pool patient effect sizes across cohorts as though they share one imaging distribution.

**Source and denominator.** TCIA `Vestibular-Schwannoma-SEG` version 2, DOI `10.7937/TCIA.9YTJ-5Q73`, CC BY 4.0. The official mapping contains 242 patients with T2 image and T2 RTSTRUCT; the NBIA MR series response exposes 241, with `VS-SEG-133` absent. Attempt exactly those 241 listed T2 patients in sorted patient-ID order, and report 242 as the mapped source denominator. No replacement patients or challenge-private test data. The first case `VS-SEG-001` may be run once as a software/model preflight under this unchanged protocol; include it in the full run and record its outcome.

**Before inference per patient.** Download only exact mapped T2 and T2 RTSTRUCT public series, preserve object SHA-256 and member hashes. Require single complete 3D MR T2 series, uniform original DICOM geometry, 1.0-1.5 mm native plane spacing/thickness, and exact same-patient RTSTRUCT Frame of Reference. Use `TV` closed planar contours referencing that patient's T2 SOPs, with plane error <=0.001 mm and tumor points inside FOV. Rasterize referenced polygons at native pixel centers. Record cases with missing/invalid labels or fewer than four eligible targets as failures. Do not select a fallback label or resize a T1 mask. T1-origin contours already interpolated into T2 RTSTRUCT remain a label limitation; an eroded-TV sensitivity and original-contour conversion comparison are subsequent checks, not a replacement target selector.

**Input and target rule.** Sort slices by physical normal. Phase-0 observed T2 planes are indices 0,4,8,...; targets have residue 1 or 3, at least 64 `TV` pixels within support and four nearby phase-0 anchors for PCHIP. Support is the prior ds007045 adjacent-anchor >1%-of-maximum-absolute-value rule. For each residue choose two sorted eligible slices at floor((n-1)/3) and floor(2(n-1)/3), requiring distinct choices. All targets, ROI counts and observed-only min/max are fixed before model scores. No phase-2 or target input to any estimator.

**Estimators and endpoint.** Use the unchanged frozen IXI HAT and SwinIR checkpoints and `prepared`/`predict` CPU functions, one Torch thread, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`. Compare each model with raw two-neighbor linear, model-pipeline linear, four-phase-0-anchor raw PCHIP and four-phase-0-anchor model-pipeline PCHIP. For the model-pipeline PCHIP, interpolate the same normalized 256-pixel observed planes, clip `[0,1]`, resize back and undo observed-only normalization. Patient means equally average four target normalized MSEs in `TV` and acquired-anchor support. **Primary cross-pathology falsifier:** HAT minus pipeline-PCHIP tumor mean difference positive with a patient-bootstrap 95% interval wholly above zero **and** full-support difference negative with its interval wholly below zero; require at least 100 completed patients and a Wilson 95% lower bound >0.20 for simultaneous tumor-worse/full-better patient signs. SwinIR, raw PCHIP/linear comparisons, CISS/TSE and 40/80-plane strata are secondary descriptions. Use 5,000 patient bootstrap draws, seed `20261006`; no target/threshold tuning. Report every source/transfer/model failure, case and target CSV, and input/checkpoint hashes. Positive results would be transferred-model regional error replication on a *different lesion type*, not glioma confirmation, causal hallucination, diagnosis loss, a new model or a deployable safety score. A failed gate stops this claim.

**Resource and independence limits.** CPU and bounded per-case downloads only; preserve at least 15 GB disk free and use at most one worker so the user's GPU training is not touched. Do not read locked IXI test data. TCIA IDs are anonymized; differing institution and diagnosis support source separation but cannot prove a universal patient-level identity crosswalk. Before publication, compare T2 RTSTRUCT masks to original `contours.json` on boundary cases, test a fixed downstream task endpoint, complete prior-art/rights/reproduction review, and keep sequence-specific effects visible.
