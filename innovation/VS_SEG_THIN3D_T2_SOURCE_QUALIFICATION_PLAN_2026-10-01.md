# VS-SEG native high-resolution T2: source qualification plan

Written after the CrossMoDA and original VS-SEG descriptors, before opening any VS-SEG patient pixels. This is a **different pathology and contrast** from the glioma `ds007045` pilot: vestibular schwannoma on 3D CISS/TSE T2 rather than glioma on clinical T2. A positive result would be a cross-pathology stress test, not a same-disease replication or a clinical harm certificate.

1. Pin TCIA `Vestibular-Schwannoma-SEG` version 2, DOI `10.7937/TCIA.9YTJ-5Q73`, CC BY 4.0, and preserve the official collection HTML/series-list/mapping bytes and hashes. Count distinct people, MR T2 series and RTSTRUCT/contour availability without decoding images. Check source identity against IXI/MSD/BraTS/`ds007045` at available identifiers and acknowledge any unverifiable anonymity.
2. Before any target pixel, choose the lexicographically first patient with exactly one documented high-resolution 3D T2 series and a same-patient RT structure or official T2-space contour file, based only on nonpixel metadata. Inspect that patient's original DICOM headers and contour coordinate metadata. Require actual 1.0-1.5 mm slice spacing/thickness and internally consistent image position/orientation; check label-to-T2 geometry and tumor presence. Retain failures, do not substitute based on model outcome.
3. Prefer the original T2-space `contours.json` plus the official repository's documented conversion route, but independently inspect how off-slice T1-origin tumor contours become a T2-grid binary mask. Compare against RTSTRUCT-derived T2 mask on one case before selecting a conversion for the full cohort. Never assume that a contour JSON is already a voxel label. The paper reports possible first/last-slice differences, so report boundary sensitivity.
4. If the source passes, freeze a cohort denominator and the x4 axis/phase, four tumor-positive withheld targets (or a declared case-ineligible rule), intensity normalization, acquired-anchor support, lesion and local control ROIs, raw/pipeline linear, equal-phase PCHIP, fixed IXI checkpoints, patient bootstrap, and a primary comparison **before** any full-cohort prediction. Reuse no outcomes to select cases or baselines. Check original native T2 versus defacing and possible resampling, with DICOM tags rather than collection-level prose alone.
5. Stop the same-grid experiment if no reliable tumor label can be mapped to the original T2 grid. CrossMoDA's unlabeled hrT2 training subset is not itself a labeled confirmation set. Do not mix its private test set with public TCIA cases or claim independence solely from a changed diagnosis name. No GPU or locked IXI test; leave the user's running training untouched.
