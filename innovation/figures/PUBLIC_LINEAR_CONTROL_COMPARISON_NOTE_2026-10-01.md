# Patient-level native-linear versus SwinIR partner control figure

[Vector PDF](public_linear_control_patient_comparison.pdf) and [220 dpi PNG](public_linear_control_patient_comparison.png) show the same patient's `Delta O` under the post-result native-linear and original SwinIR partners, with the IXI-trained HAT reference, target and support held fixed. The panels are separate MSValid RRMS (84 patients) and TCIA ReMIND preoperative T2 (62 patients) analyses. Points are anonymous patients; the white diamond is the within-source mean and the dashed line is equality. The figure does **not** pool cohorts or establish a neural-network causal contribution, because `O` has a partner-specific MSE denominator and partner error quality differs.

The [plot script](plot_public_linear_control_comparison.py) joins the original and [linear-control](../PUBLIC_LINEAR_PARTNER_CONTROL_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) patient CSVs by internal source ID, requires an exact patient-set match, checks patient counts and finite values, and records both input SHA-256 hashes in the [source manifest](public_linear_control_patient_comparison_sources.json). Patient IDs, image pixels and raw imaging data are not drawn or embedded in the figure. The PNG was visually inspected for readable labels and overlap. Suggested caption: "For each fixed patient, a native-linear companion retains a positive shared-versus-disjoint published-slice context contrast. The original SwinIR companion shows a larger contrast for the tested pair, but the two endpoints use different partner errors and denominators; their difference is not an identified network effect. MSValid was a same-patient follow-up, whereas ReMIND was a later new-source frozen test; both linear controls were post-result. ReMIND published DICOMs carry an MPR flag."
