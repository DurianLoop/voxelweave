# Three-source patient-level observability figure

The [220 dpi PNG](public_three_source_observability.png) shows the frozen IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR pair on MSValid (84 patients, 479 targets), ReMIND (62, 248) and processed UPENN-GBM DICOM T2 (374, 1,496). Each gray segment connects one patient's shared and disjoint published-context scores; white diamonds mark patient-equal means. No patient identifiers or images appear. The panels retain the same 0-40% vertical axis; their color is only a source key. The three displayed intervals come from the individual frozen cohort summaries, **not** a pooled three-source test.

The [renderer](plot_public_three_source_observability.py) reads only the three frozen per-patient CSVs and summaries, checks patient and target counts and recomputes each displayed mean. The [source manifest](public_three_source_observability_sources.json) stores SHA-256 hashes of all six inputs and the rendered PNG. Visual inspection at full figure size found no clipping or overlap. The UPENN source is a later, informed test after two positive cohorts; all 374 selected DICOM series are DERIVED/SECONDARY/OTHER. MSValid is a same-patient weight-pair follow-up; ReMIND is the later protocol-frozen source test and its DICOM carries MPR. These design histories must accompany the figure.

Suggested caption: "For one fixed IXI-trained model pair, switching the companion from shared to disjoint released through-plane context increased retrospective paired-error visibility in all three analyzed public-source strata. Lines are paired patients, not independent targets. The cohorts differ in selection chronology and release processing; the UPENN series are derived, and ReMIND carries MPR. The comparison does not identify original acquisition effects, improve paired reconstruction MSE on UPENN, or validate clinical decisions."
