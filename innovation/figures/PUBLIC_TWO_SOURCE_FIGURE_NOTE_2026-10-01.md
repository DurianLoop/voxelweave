# Public-source patient-level observability figure

[Vector PDF](public_two_source_observability.pdf) and [220 dpi PNG](public_two_source_observability.png) show paired patient-level observable-error percentages for the public-source IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR weight pair. Left: MSValid RRMS, 84 patients/479 targets, a **same-patient post-result weight follow-up**. Right: TCIA ReMIND preoperative T2, 62 patients/248 targets, a later protocol-frozen new source test. Gray lines connect each patient's shared and disjoint contexts; filled dots are patients, white diamonds are patient means. Intervals are the respective original patient bootstrap intervals; they are not a pooled cross-disease comparison. The ReMIND DICOM MPR limitation and context/geometry confounding must accompany any use of the figure.

The [plot script](plot_public_two_source_observability.py) reads only `experiments/msvalid_ixi_weights_secondary/per_patient.csv` and `experiments/remind_native_t2_ixi_pair/per_patient.csv` plus their summaries, verifies patient counts and main means, and stores input SHA-256 in [source manifest](public_two_source_observability_sources.json). No raw image pixels, patient identifiers or fastMRI/NingXia data are included in the rendered figure. The final one-page PDF was rendered at 150 dpi and visually checked for clipping/overlap. PDF SHA-256 `f57c84e5ba80ea293df7a74274edc4676ecc022920273bb20d0db749f3266360`; PNG SHA-256 `74e6b9e2396dd77bc3eba72ffe6f5913ac4c05832188b174539c7f0c7bff57c4`.

Suggested caption: "Shared published-slice context conceals paired reconstruction error in two separately sourced brain T2 cohorts for one fixed IXI-trained HAT/SwinIR pair. Each line represents one patient, averaged within target residue before patient weighting. MSValid is a same-patient follow-up devised after an earlier fastMRI-weight result; ReMIND is a later new-source test frozen before its pixels were decoded. The source contrasts are not a randomized disease comparison, and the published ReMIND DICOMs carry an MPR flag."
