The proposed algorithm does not require any calibration
data for estimation of coil sensitivities or linear filters Qc.
Computationally, this approach is orders of magnitude faster
than CLEAR since the unrolled algorithm requires fewer
iterations to converge. In addition, it eliminates the need for
performing singular value decomposition (SVD) of Γc every
iteration, which increases the computational complexity of
CLEAR.
Apart from the multi-channel annihilation relations (5)
resulting from the multi-channel acquisition, MR images also
satisfy annihilation properties resulting from the redundancy
of patches. For instance, smooth regions can be annihilated
by derivative filters, while wavelet filters are effective in
annihilating texture regions. The proposed image domain
multi-channel annihilation CNN framework can exploit these
annihilation relations as well, which is a distinction from the
k-space approach in [17]; the larger number of annihilation
relations can translate to improved performance. Note that the
traditional CLEAR approach only exploits the multi-channel
relations in (5).
B. Joint Reconstruction-Segmentation Framework
MRI reconstruction and segmentation are often studied
as two different tasks in the MR image analysis pipeline.
In the case of a direct cascade of tasks, any aliasing or
blurring artifact in reconstructions due to highly accelerated
acquisition gets propagated to the segmentation task, leading
to degradation of segmentation quality [22]. In this section,
we propose a multi-task framework for joint reconstruction
and segmentation to obtain segmentations that are robust to

encourage the learning of common latent variables for both
the segmentation and reconstruction tasks, thus exploiting the
synergy between them. In particular, we hypothesize that the
spatial domain features extracted by the encoder NIE from
multiple channels would become sharper due to the auxiliary
segmentation loss, resulting in improved representation of
edges and finer details. The shared encoder architecture also
facilitates the learning of the segmentation task from a few
labelled datasets as explained below.
