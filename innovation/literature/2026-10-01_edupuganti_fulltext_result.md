I read the abstract, introduction, relevant method passages on VAE sampling, data consistency and SURE, empirical evaluation, table/figure captions and conclusions. The XML omits some displayed equation content, so this is **not** an independent derivation check or a visual verification of every figure. No code or numeric experiment was reproduced.

**Method and data.** A VAE samples multiple reconstructions conditioned on the same zero-filled **single-coil undersampled k-space** observation, with measured frequencies restored by a data-consistency projection. Monte Carlo output variance is compared with squared bias/error, which require the full-sampling reference for evaluation. The paper emphasizes that variance alone can miss dominant bias. For target-free risk estimation, it applies density-compensated SURE to approximate MSE under a zero-mean Gaussian residual assumption and an estimated residual variance; its Jacobian term measures input sensitivity and can also be applied to deterministic networks under the assumptions. The experiments use proton-density fat-saturated knee MRI from **19 patients**, split by patient, with retrospectively applied variable-density undersampling; there is no external brain T2 test. The full-text table of undersampling rates reports SURE--MSE R-squared of `0.97, 0.90, 0.92, 0.84` for 2x, 4x, 8x and 16x respectively. These are within this knee task and are not MIRA `Delta O` values or clinical calibration guarantees.

**Collision decision.** MRI model-output variation, the variance-versus-bias distinction, input-response sensitivity and target-free risk estimation were established before MIRA. Any claim that MIRA first shows ensemble disagreement can miss common error, or first audits reconstruction risk without a target, is false. This work does **not** compare a fixed brain through-plane interpolation target/reference with a companion prediction switched between shared and disjoint **released physical slice sets**; it uses a known Fourier forward operator, a single observation and posterior latent samples. Its SURE route is not a drop-in baseline for the project's native-slice x4 input without a defensible noise/forward model, but the manuscript must cite it when distinguishing retrospective `O` from actionable error estimation. The current empirical context-switch distinction survives this reading, while global novelty and the Kuestner 2024 method-level collision remain unproved.
