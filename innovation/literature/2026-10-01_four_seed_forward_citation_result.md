# Four-seed forward-citation audit and MRI task-level comparators

This is a bounded prior-art audit for the frozen IXI HAT/SwinIR same-target, released-through-plane-context result, not a systematic review or proof of priority. The [prewritten query](2026-10-01_mira_four_seed_citation_audit_plan.md), [raw OpenAlex responses and index](search_snapshots/mira_four_seed_citations_2026-10-01/index.json), and [arXiv version index](search_snapshots/mira_four_seed_citations_2026-10-01/arxiv_candidates/index.json) preserve the retrievable inputs. Hu 2021, Kuestner 2024, NPB-REC 2022 and Radmanesh 2022 had respectively 11, 19, 0 and 32 indexed forward citations: 62 returned records, **61 distinct OpenAlex work IDs**. The zero for NPB-REC is an OpenAlex citation-link result, not evidence of no real citations. All returned titles were screened; plausible methods were escalated to abstracts and the two accessible full texts below. Each query was capped at 100 newest records, although none reached that cap. Unciting papers and indexing gaps remain outside the screen.

We have not rerun its code or verified every figure value independently. | Directly blocks broad language that multiple MRI reconstructions first reveal missed pathology or that pixel metrics alone establish clinical value. | *Evaluating Structural Uncertainty in Accelerated MRI: Are Voxelwise Measures Useful Surrogates?*, arXiv `2503.10527v2` (2025 preprint; OpenAlex indexes a 2026 LNCS DOI `10.1007/978-3-032-09513-8_49`); [archived HTML full text](source_cache/mira_four_seed_fulltext_routes_2026-10-01/structural_uncertainty_arxiv_html.html), [extracted text](extracted/structural_uncertainty_arxiv_html.txt). | Uses 20 Calgary-Campinas validation subjects with 5x undersampled T1 k-space, same input within each seed ensemble: 15 UNet, 11 VN and 11 RIM models. The two HTML full texts show different interventions from MIRA, but narrow non-overlap in this read set is not a global novelty certificate. [Kuestner 2024](2026-10-01_kuestner_fulltext_access_audit.md) is especially relevant and still only readable at abstract level; a later paper's description of it is not a substitute for its original methods. arXiv PDF transfers stopped at about 3 MiB and HTTP Range returned 406; no incomplete PDF was archived. The [route log](source_cache/mira_four_seed_fulltext_routes_2026-10-01/index.json) records successful HTML responses and failed full-text attempts. Do not label the abstract-only works or inaccessible Kuestner as methods-level exclusions.

The defensible distinction remains a **retrospective, source- and fixed-model-specific paired-error audit under a released-plane context switch**. A stronger paper would need either documented original independent measurements to identify a physical mechanism, or credible labels on the exact withheld target planes to show task consequences. The present three-source result establishes neither clinical safety nor a deployable uncertainty score. Next literature gate: acquire Kuestner through a lawful library/author route and run a target-venue backward/forward search including the clinical-task papers above; revise the novelty sentence if an equivalent same-target intervention appears.
