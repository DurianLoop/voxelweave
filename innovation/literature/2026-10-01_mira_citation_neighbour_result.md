# MIRA citation-neighbour and recent-work collision screen

This is a bounded, **non-systematic** prior-art check against the present fixed-target published-slice-context audit. The [frozen query plan](2026-10-01_mira_citation_neighbour_plan.md) yielded 11 OpenAlex-indexed works citing Hu et al. 2021 and three first pages of 50 recent results, for **157 unique works** after deduplication. The exact raw JSON responses, query URLs, counts, retrieval time and SHA-256 values are in the [search index](search_snapshots/mira_citation_neighbours_2026-10-01/index.json). All 157 titles were screened; abstracts of plausible MRI reconstruction/error/uncertainty papers were reviewed. OpenAlex relevance ranking included many unrelated studies, and the latter three queries reported 5,884, 2,180 and 1,312 hits while only the first 50 of each were examined. Absence of a direct collision here is therefore weak evidence, not a priority certificate.

The strongest currently defensible distinction is **a source- and model-specific, same-target retrospective audit of how changing a companion interpolator's released through-plane slice context alters paired-error visibility**. This is an empirical protocol/result, not a new theorem, reconstruction model, deployable error predictor, pure acquisition-identity intervention or proven global first. The 2024 deep-ensemble full text and a venue-specific forward/backward citation search remain open. If that paper contains a materially equivalent mask/context-switch experiment, the novelty sentence must be revised before submission.
