# Frozen four-seed MRI error-audit citation screen

Before retrieval, use the OpenAlex public API to resolve four DOI seeds and fetch at most the first 100 newest citing works for each. Seeds: Hu et al. MICCAI 2021 `10.1007/978-3-030-87199-4_57`; Kuestner et al. MRM 2024 `10.1002/mrm.30030`; NPB-REC arXiv 2022 `10.48550/arXiv.2208.03966`; Radmanesh et al. Radiology: AI 2022 `10.1148/ryai.210313`. API filter: `cites:<seed OpenAlex ID>`, sort `publication_date:desc`, `per-page=100`. Record exact URLs, raw response bytes and hashes, total counts, DOI, year, venue, title and any indexed abstract. Keep failures and metadata gaps.

Screen all distinct returned titles, then abstracts of plausible MRI reconstruction error, interpolation, ensemble, input-perturbation, or acquisition-context methods. Escalate to full text any paper that may hold a target/reference fixed, switch another estimator's real measurement/context set, and measure paired error/disagreement against withheld truth. This is a **bounded forward-citation audit**, not a systematic review: forward citations omit unciting work, OpenAlex coverage is incomplete, seed DOI linkage can fail, and first-page caps remain. An apparent absence of exact collision will not certify priority or publication readiness.
