# Prior-art check for the MSD same-neighbor linear failure observation

This is a focused rereading of archived accessible articles and their extracted text on 2026-10-01, not a new systematic search or global novelty certificate. All papers use different data and forward tasks from the project's registered-release x4 T2 slice withholding, so their numerical outcomes cannot be pooled with [our result](../MSD_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_LINEAR_AUDIT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md).

| Paper / reading level | Directly checked contract and result | Boundary for the new finding |
|---|---|---|
| Radmanesh et al., *Exploring the Acceleration Limits of Deep Learning Variational Network-based Two-dimensional Brain MRI*, Radiology: AI 2022, DOI `10.1148/ryai.210313`; archived [PMC full text](extracted/rsna_acceleration_limits_2022_pmc.txt), methods/results/discussion reread. | FastMRI 2D brain k-space model, 69 radiologist-reviewed T2/T2-FLAIR/T1 volumes with normal/abnormal enrichment; subtle abnormalities could be pseudonormalized at high acceleration. An organ-shift knee check gave lower SSIM than a knee-trained E2E-VarNet (4x 0.8942 vs 0.9302; 8x 0.8436 vs 0.8920). | Abnormality fidelity and out-of-distribution degradation are known issues. It does not compare x4 T2 slice interpolators against raw two-neighbor linear on BRaTS tumor masks. Our MSE is not radiologist diagnostic fidelity. |
| Li et al., *SliceBridge*, arXiv `2609.01827` (2026); [complete PDF](source_cache/targeted_recent_fulltexts_2026-10-01/slicebridge_2026.pdf) and [text](extracted/slicebridge_2026.txt), sections 3.2-4.3 reread. | Trained on multi-source T1, externally tested on 581 IXI T1 cases. The explicit two-boundary linear baseline had SSIM 0.931 and PSNR 28.43 dB for one missing layer; SliceBridge had 0.960 and 32.53 dB. In 448 eligible controlled-corruption cases, median affected-ROI volume error was 1.30% for linear and 1.05% for SliceBridge, although its matched uncoupled-flow comparison was null after Holm correction (`p=0.370`). | Learned interpolation can beat linear under a different training/target contract; our finding cannot be generalized as a universal learned-model failure. It also makes two-neighbor linear and downstream task baselines mandatory for any proposed improvement. |
| Verschuur et al., *Improved neonatal brain MRI segmentation by interpolation of motion corrupted slices*, Journal of Neuroimaging 2022, DOI `10.1111/jon.12985`; [PMC full text](extracted/verschuur_neonatal_t2_2022.txt), methods/results/limitations reread. | Compared linear, spline and shape-preserving spline on withheld neonatal T2 slices; shape-preserving spline had the highest pilot SSIM. Real corrupted-slice interpolation affected automatic tissue segmentation, and the authors warn that small lesions may be lost and advise against diagnosis from interpolated images. | T2 interpolation, simple analytic baselines and downstream segmentation consequences have direct precedent. Its neonatal motion setting and label task differ from BRaTS release-space tumor MSE. |
| Fischer, Kuestner and Baumgartner 2023, [arXiv full-text audit](2026-10-01_fischer_mri_uq_fulltext_result.md). | PHiRec samples accelerated knee MRI reconstructions and propagates variation to a segmentation U-Net, including acceleration shift. | Reconstruction-to-task uncertainty is established; our fixed-target pixel MSE does not measure segmentation risk or claim this concept. |
| Morshuis et al. 2025, [arXiv HTML full-text audit](2026-10-01_four_seed_forward_citation_result.md). | *Mind the Detail* optimizes semantically diverse fastMRI+ knee reconstructions and evaluates pathology recall/mAP with an independent detector. | Pixel-region MSE is not pathology recall, and a warning about missing pathology is not novel by itself. |
| *The Challenge of Rendering Abnormalities Accurately Using Deep Learning-Based Reconstruction in Accelerated Brain MRI*, DOI `10.1097/rli.0000000000001308`; [indexed abstract only](2026-10-01_four_seed_forward_citation_result.md). | The available abstract reports faster abnormal-region degradation than matched normal regions in accelerated fastMRI brain, with radiologist abnormality boxes. Methods and numeric tables were not obtained here. | A broad “global score hides abnormality loss” novelty claim is already strongly challenged; full text and exact ROI/baseline definitions are required before any method-level distinction. |
| Kuestner et al. 2024, DOI `10.1002/mrm.30030`, [access audit](2026-10-01_kuestner_fulltext_access_audit.md). | Formal abstract describes deep-ensemble uncertainty under shifts including anatomy and pathology. Methods/full text remain unavailable in this environment. | Do not claim method-level non-overlap or priority until a lawful full text is read. |
| Yang and Wang, *SafeRestore*, arXiv `2609.03475v1` (2026); [archived preprint text](extracted/SafeRestore_2026.txt), abstract, methods, results and limitations reread. | In industrial inspection, a fixed raw/bicubic/learned restoration action set and review option are ranked for two detector-relative harms, then a gate is tested on disjoint certification data. Only one of five primary training repetitions passed certification; simpler fixed actions often passed more. | A raw-linear fallback or selective restoration policy is not by itself a new idea. This is not MRI or through-plane reconstruction and its risk certificate does not transfer to this cohort. |

Scientific decision: the current 483-case paired baseline result is valuable as a **negative control** for the project's own claims. The [later MSD full-support replay](../MSD_FULL_SUPPORT_EQUAL_NEIGHBOR_POSTRESULT_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) shows that tumor ROI disadvantage can coexist with a mean global SwinIR advantage, but this broad pattern also has abnormal-region MRI precedents. It does not yet isolate architecture, training distribution or pathology harm. A potential publishable direction would need a predeclared, source-independent benchmark with matched preprocessing/observation budgets and a genuinely task-level endpoint or a falsifiable mechanism that improves failure prediction. The current records do not satisfy those gates.
