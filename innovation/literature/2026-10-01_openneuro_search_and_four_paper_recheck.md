# Bounded source search and four-paper recheck

The official OpenNeuro GraphQL type probes and seven fixed search responses are archived with query strings and SHA-256 in [the candidate index](source_cache/openneuro_glioma_candidates_2026-10-01/index.json). Five ordinary `search` queries (`glioma`, `brain tumor`, `brain tumour`, `T2w lesion`, `glioblastoma`) each returned HTTP 200 with `data.search=null` and no GraphQL error. Two `advancedSearch` diagnosis queries returned an empty edge list. These outcomes do **not** establish that OpenNeuro has no qualifying dataset: the ordinary search endpoint gave no usable list and the advanced diagnosis field may depend on catalog indexing. No image bytes were fetched. The existing directly enumerated `ds007045` cohort remains the only qualified high-resolution lead in this archive.

Four archived full texts were reread against the current single-T2 x4 claim, using the source copies/text listed below. This is targeted reading, not a systematic novelty review.

| Source and reading | Constraint on the present innovation idea |
|---|---|
| Verschuur et al., *Journal of Neuroimaging* 2022, DOI `10.1111/jon.12985`, [PMC-derived full text](extracted/verschuur_neonatal_t2_2022.txt), methods/results/discussion | Direct T2 missing-slice work compares linear, spline and four-neighbor shape-preserving cubic interpolation, then examines automatic segmentation. Its neonatal motion setting differs from adult glioma, but a new tumor-region study needs a shape-preserving analytic baseline. It forbids presenting PCHIP itself or T2 interpolation-to-segmentation as a new method. |
| Li et al., *SliceBridge*, arXiv:2609.01827 (2026), [complete PDF](source_cache/targeted_recent_fulltexts_2026-10-01/slicebridge_2026.pdf), method/context and matched downstream results | T1 interval restoration already uses many intact neighboring slices, positional maps, coupled flow and a matched uncoupled-flow control. The paper reports no significant matched downstream volume-error benefit over uncoupled flow after Holm correction (`p=0.370`). Smoothness or pixel ranking alone does not establish clinical task benefit. Its 32 intact context slices are not the present four-sparse-anchor contract. |
| Savant, *MRI Slice Interpolation: Problem Formulation Dominates Architecture*, arXiv 2026 [archived PDF](Savant_2026_MRI_Slice_Interpolation_Problem_Formulation.pdf), abstract/method/limitations | In prostate MRI, moving input neighbors closer strongly changes model scores. This is a direct warning to freeze physical context and equal information budgets. It is not a same-target, equal-spacing companion-context error-alignment audit, and its reported gain cannot be transferred to this brain T2 setting. |
| Fischer, Kuestner and Baumgartner, *Uncertainty Estimation and Propagation in Accelerated MRI Reconstruction*, arXiv:2308.02631v1 (2023), [full HTML text](source_cache/fischer_mri_uq_2026-10-01/article.txt), abstract/method/task portions | Probabilistic MRI reconstruction uncertainty propagated to segmentation already exists. The current post-hoc lesion MSE difference has no calibration, segmentation-risk or deployable uncertainty claim. This paper does not settle the inaccessible Kuestner et al. 2024 method-level collision. |

The latest [PCHIP sensitivity](../DS007045_THIN3D_PCHIP_SENSITIVITY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) confirms only a narrower HAT-specific, processed-grid regional error discordance in the same source. The strongest currently supported manuscript route remains the retrospective public-source MIRA measurement audit; its phase/context confounds, simple interpolation control, clean-environment reproduction and publication rights still require explicit treatment. A second native high-resolution T2 source and task endpoint are prepared as follow-up gates rather than claimed results.
