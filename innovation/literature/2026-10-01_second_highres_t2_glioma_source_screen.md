# Second high-resolution T2 glioma-label source: bounded screen

Question: can an independent public cohort confirm the exploratory HAT tumor/full-error sign reversal on genuinely high-resolution T2 rather than 2D thick slices upsampled into 1-mm MNI volumes? This is a targeted search, not a systematic review. The [four-query metadata snapshot](source_cache/highres_t2_tumor_source_search_2026-10-01/index.json) includes two 25-record Europe PMC results and 20 Crossref records; a 25-record DataCite response exceeded the 1-MB cap and was discarded. No images, GPU, locked IXI test, or access agreement were used.

| Dataset and full-text depth | Relevant published facts | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Amsterdam IMAGO, *Scientific Data* 2026, DOI `10.1038/s41597-026-07424-8`, [Europe PMC full text](source_cache/imago_2026_descriptor/index.json) | First release has 500 preoperative patients; 249 preoperative and 190 postoperative nnUNet-generated, neuroradiologist-accepted segmentations. All four MRI modalities are aligned and resampled to 1-mm MNI. The methods explicitly state **"T2w images were scanned as 2D multislice"** even though the inclusion paragraph says four structural sequences were preferably 3D. | Not an independent native high-resolution T2 source. Its large case count and 1-mm released affine do not override the explicit 2D acquisition statement. No image download. |
| Erasmus Glioma Database (EGD), *Data in Brief* 2021, DOI `10.1016/j.dib.2021.107191`, [Europe PMC full text](source_cache/egd_2021_descriptor/index.json) | 774 preoperative glioma patients, 374 manually annotated whole-tumor masks and 400 automatically generated masks. Manual masks were drawn on the original T2 **or** T2-FLAIR before atlas registration; a supplied table identifies the basis and observer. Released images are registered to 1-mm MNI. The paper describes diverse clinical protocols but supplies no per-patient original T2 slice-thickness distribution in its text. | Potentially stronger human lesion labels, but not yet a qualified high-resolution source. The [official XNAT metadata probe](source_cache/egd_official_metadata_2026-10-01/index.json) returned HTTP 200 for project information and HTTP 403 for a five-subject listing. Data access and original T2 geometry remain unresolved; no agreement was accepted or bypassed. |

The search also surfaced many BraTS segmentation methods that reuse existing registered images; a `3D` network or 1-mm atlas grid is not evidence that the **T2 acquisition** was 3D/high-resolution. Neither read dataset closes the second-source gate. The current ds007045 34-person thin-3D lead remains single-source and concentrated at one site. A future source must expose per-case original T2 acquisition type/thickness, exact image/label alignment, independent identity and usable access terms **before** target-pixel confirmation. HAT's secondary pilot signal cannot be upgraded by adding IMAGO's known 2D T2 or by assuming EGD raw resolution from its processed MNI grid.
