# Recent through-plane and task-aware MRI full-text audit

The [pre-retrieval plan](2026-10-01_targeted_recent_mri_screen_plan.md) used four 2024-2026 OpenAlex queries, capped at 50 newest records each. [Raw responses and the deduplicated index](search_snapshots/targeted_recent_mri_2026-10-01/index.json) contain 200 returned records and 163 unique work IDs; all returned titles and plausible abstracts were screened. The queries' total counts were 1,434, 5,430, 1,479 and 3,401, so the newest-first cap captured a small and noisy fraction, largely 2026 work. This is discovery, not a sensitive systematic search or proof of priority. Two relevant articles were obtained as complete, official/open PDFs with exact URLs and hashes in the [source index](source_cache/targeted_recent_fulltexts_2026-10-01/index.json). Their [extracted texts](extracted/slicebridge_2026.txt) and [glioma text](extracted/glioma_task_aware_uq_2026.txt) support the checks below; the key numeric tables were also visually inspected on source PDF pages 8 and 15, respectively.

| Paper and actual experiment | Result checked in full text | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Li et al., *SliceBridge: context-consistent repair of corrupted slice intervals in T1-weighted MRI*, arXiv:2609.01827 (2026), [PDF](source_cache/targeted_recent_fulltexts_2026-10-01/slicebridge_2026.pdf). A rectified-flow model fills one or more withheld/corrupted axial slices from 16 intact context slices on either side. Its matched uncoupled-flow comparator gets the **same context, data, architecture and position information** but generates slices independently. Development used 9,877 ADNI/AOMIC/NACC/OASIS-3 T1 volumes, with 581 IXI T1 subjects held out for external evaluation. | Versus uncoupled flow, interval coupling reduced internal z-gradient MAE by 32.9%-41.3% across interval lengths 2-12; all 581 paired cases favored it on that metric. In controlled corruptions, 448/581 cases had eligible affected ROIs. Median affected-ROI volume APE was 1.95% for corrupted volumes and 1.05% for both flow variants; **SliceBridge versus uncoupled flow was not significant (Holm-adjusted p=0.370)**. The reference was an uncorrupted preprocessed volume and the downstream segmentation was SLANT-TICV, not an independent manual tumor annotation. Real corruption examples lacked clean truth. | Direct near-neighbor for published-slice restoration, explicit context geometry, through-plane consistency and downstream task evaluation. It does **not** switch one estimator to disjoint published observations while keeping a second estimator and the target fixed, or measure paired-error visibility. MIRA must not claim first through-plane context reconstruction, first IXI external slice restoration, or that better through-plane consistency necessarily improves task outcome. The non-significant matched downstream comparison particularly cautions against inferring clinical value from MIRA's `Delta O`. |
| Mosquera Rojas et al., *Towards Trustworthy AI for Glioma Diagnosis: A Task-Aware Evaluation of Uncertainty Quantification*, Machine Learning for Biomedical Imaging 2026, DOI `10.59275/j.melba.2026-456d`, [PDF](source_cache/targeted_recent_fulltexts_2026-10-01/glioma_task_aware_uq_2026.pdf). Multi-task MRI model jointly segments tumor and predicts IDH, 1p/19q and grade. It compares Monte Carlo dropout, deep ensembles and their combination, then tests calibration, case error detection and selective prediction against available labels. Test data include 133 TCGA-GBM and 103 TCGA-LGG cases; training spans multiple glioma sources. | At a DSC <=0.7 segmentation-error definition, predicted-tumor aggregated predictive uncertainty had U-AUC 0.95 [0.92,0.98], AP 0.82 [0.72,0.91], Lift 4.95 [3.89,6.67] and AURC 0.13 [0.12,0.14] for MCD. Segmentation error-rate sensitivity was also checked at DSC thresholds 0.60, 0.70 and 0.80. The authors found a combined segmentation/classification trust score did not consistently beat task-specific classification uncertainty. | Strong task-aware and operational MRI UQ precedent. It is **not a reconstruction experiment** and does not establish a same-target released-slice context intervention. MIRA's retrospective `O` needs withheld truth and cannot be compared numerically with these deployable uncertainty metrics. Any future lesion extension must report an explicit task endpoint, case denominator, calibration/ranking baseline and matched information budget; `Delta O` alone is insufficient. |

**Novelty decision:** the narrow fixed-reference, switched-companion released-context estimand has no exact match in these two full texts, but broad slice-restoration, context-awareness, structural consistency and task-risk novelty is already occupied. One abstract-only related article, Kuestner et al. 2024 DOI `10.1002/mrm.30030`, still needs a lawful methods-level copy. A new direct request to OpenAlex's indexed content XML URL returned HTTP 401; the ordinary proxy endpoint refused connections, while direct metadata API access worked. The 401 is an access condition, not evidence about the paper's content. The [earlier access audit](2026-10-01_kuestner_fulltext_access_audit.md) remains the authority for all other attempted routes. No unauthorized access, GPU use or patient-pixel read occurred.

**Experiment consequence:** the most useful next upgrade remains a predeclared tumor-region reconstruction endpoint on verified same-grid UPENN atlas NIfTI T2 and expert mask pairs, with a non-tumor control and all absent pairs counted. The official NIfTI package still lacks a file-level inventory in this environment; the [space/eligibility gate](../UPENN_GBM_NIFTI_LABEL_FEASIBILITY_RESULT_2026-10-01.md) applies. Do not overlay those masks on the existing processed DICOM cohort. A method claim based on interval coupling would additionally have to beat SliceBridge's same-context uncoupled-flow and simple interpolation contracts, including its null matched downstream result.
