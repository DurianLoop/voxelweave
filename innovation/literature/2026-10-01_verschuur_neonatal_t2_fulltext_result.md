# Verschuur 2022: T2 slice interpolation and segmentation full-text audit

Verschuur et al., *Improved neonatal brain MRI segmentation by interpolation of motion corrupted slices*, Journal of Neuroimaging 2022, DOI `10.1111/jon.12985`, has a readable PMC BioC [full text](source_cache/verschuur_neonatal_t2_2022_bioc.xml). The exact NCBI endpoint, 90,946-byte response, SHA-256 and 140 extracted passages are preserved in the [source index](source_cache/verschuur_neonatal_t2_2022_index.json); [plain extracted text](extracted/verschuur_neonatal_t2_2022.txt) was used for the methods/results check. The Utwente repository PDF indexed by OpenAlex returned HTTP 403 here, so the PMC full text is the inspected source.

The study enrolled moderate/late preterm infants in the BIMP prospective cohort, using 3T T2 scans at term-equivalent age. It retrospectively replaces visually identified motion-affected 2D slices with intensity-based shape-preserving cubic-spline interpolation, then runs MANTiS segmentation of eight brain structures. The paper includes 27 no-motion controls and 33 moderate/severe motion scans. It tests clean-slice withholding in controls (18 of 54 axial slices), SSIM, Dice against the automatically segmented clean controls, and tissue-volume sensitivity/bias. Among motion scans, 25 had visually improved segmentation after interpolation; eight with adjacent corrupted slices did not improve and were excluded from quantitative analysis. The authors report average SSIM 0.895 and structure Dice 0.87-0.97 in their validation, while acknowledging blurring, infratentorial double boundaries and volume bias. Real motion-affected scans had no clean repeat target, and the control reference segmentations were automated rather than an independent manual truth.

**Collision:** This is a direct precedent for T2 through-plane interpolation followed by segmentation evaluation and for the fact that bad neighboring context can make interpolation fail. A generic claim that our project first uses interpolation to rescue MRI segmentation, first links neighbor quality to downstream tissue measurements, or first validates image-space T2 slice interpolation against a segmentation endpoint is untenable. It does not fix one reconstruction/target and switch a *second estimator's* released context to measure visibility of **paired** errors; it also does not establish our original-acquisition-identity mechanism. If a future UPENN atlas-space tumor experiment is run, cite this work and compare against a same-information shape-preserving cubic interpolator, while reporting adjacent-context and lesion-boundary failures rather than only favorable global metrics.

This comparison does not prove the current MIRA claim is globally novel. The [Kuestner access gate](2026-10-01_kuestner_fulltext_access_audit.md) and venue-targeted review remain open.
