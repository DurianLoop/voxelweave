5.4.2. Discount Factor In table 5, we present the impact of the discount factor on
L2S. Our proposed L2S method achieves the best reconstruction performance when
γ = 1. In contrast, previous dynamic sampling methods with dense-reward POMDP,
as described in [12] and [11], have shown their optimal performance at discount factors
of 0.5 and 0.9, respectively. The contrastive results make sense as the proposed sparse-
reward POMDP is explicitly designed to get the reward at the end of the trajectory,
thereby learning a long-sighted policy.
