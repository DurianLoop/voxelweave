However, our work has two limitations. Firstly, the alternating training
framework has no theoretical guarantee of convergence. Secondly, within the actual
exploration mechanism, there is a lack of diversity in the policy’s actions towards the
sample to solve (4.14), which is a fundamental challenge associated with the sparse-
reward POMDP. Through empirical observation, we have identified that as the length
of the sample increases, the complexity of training a sparse-reward POMDP escalates
significantly. In sparse-reward POMDP, the paucity of frequent informative feedback
limits the effective exploration from the environment. However, current reinforcement
learning algorithms struggle to fully mitigate these issues. In future work, we plan to
address these limitations and adapt our approach to realistic MRI scanning problems.
