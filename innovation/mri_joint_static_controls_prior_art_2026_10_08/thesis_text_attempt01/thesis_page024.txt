2.1.3 Relaxation
After applying the RF pulse, the system will gradually return to equilibrium, whereM0
is aligned withB0. This will require the nuclei to re-emit the energy that they absorbed
during the excitation phase. The relaxation is governed by two main components,
namely alongitudinal relaxation, characterized by a time constantT1, and atransverse
relaxation, characterized byT2.
