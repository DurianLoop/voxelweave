For many classes of submodular functionsh, this result is the best that can be achieved

Although the approximation guarantee cannot be generally improved, many works have

describe in the next section. These algorithms are thelazy greedy(Minoux, 1978) and
stochastic greedy, called lazier than lazy greedy (Mirzasoleiman et al., 2015).
