5.6. DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION
Bibliographic note
For the lazy LBCS work5, the work is mostly due to Baran Gözcü. The author of this
dissertation contributed to the formalization of the lazy LBCS algorithm and to part
of the 2D multi-coil experiments. Convinced of the potential of LBCS, Volkan Cevher
provided the drive to further validate and extend the method.
For the stochastic LBCS work6, Ruud van Heeswijk acquired the cardiac data, Efe Ilıcak
and Tolga Çukur provided the vocal tract data. Baran Gözcü was mostly involved in
supervising the project. Armin Eftekhari provided Propositions 1 and 2, discussed in the
previous chapter.
