7.1.1 Generative adversarial networks (GANs)
We will begin by describing what GANs are and how they work. GANs aregenerative
models: given training data{x1,..., xm}∼ p(x), they aim at learning to generate new
samples that look like they have been generated by the distributionp(x). Several deep
learning approaches have been explored to tackle the problem of generation, which we
will discuss in Section 7.5, and GANs have shined in particular by their ability to handle
multi-modal outputs (Goodfellow, 2016).
