7.3 Comparison with Zhang et al. (2019b)
We have mentioned several times until now the conceptual similarities with the approach
of Zhang et al. (2019b) with our approach. We have shown in Section 7.1.4 that their
approach isnot generative, but aims at learning the mean and variance of the posterior
distribution by minimizing a NLL (Equation 7.8). These statistics do not allow to directly
perform adaptive sampling, as we show in Appendix B.2.2.
This is why, in order to perform adaptive sampling, Zhang et al. (2019b) also train
with their reconstructor an additional model, called anevaluator eϕ(ˆx,ω ), that tries to
predict the line-wise error in Fourier domain. The role of the evaluator is not only to
provide guidance for adaptive sampling, similarly to the variance in Fourier space in our
case, but it also helps to refine the reconstruction, by adding an adversary that aims at
discriminate between real reconstructed lines and reconstructed ones.
