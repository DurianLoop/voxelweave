7.4.1 MRI adaptive sampling
Experimental setting
We carried out experiments on Fourier sampling with the same conditions as outlined in
Section 6.3
Dataset. We used again the fastMRI (Zbontar et al., 2019) single-coil knee dataset for
the experiments, using complex data and the same data normalization as previously. We
cropped the data to256× 256 before resizing them to128× 128. We used horizontal
sampling masks, and experimented among other things with the mask distribution of
Zhang et al. (2019b). This corresponds to thec+rhz setting in the previous chapter.
Recall that for the mask distribution, the setting of Zhang et al. (2019b) meaning the
model was trained with 10 center frequencies always selected and between 3 and 37
randomly selected elements. The data were postprocessed to have the magnitude of the
ground truth in the range[0, 1].
