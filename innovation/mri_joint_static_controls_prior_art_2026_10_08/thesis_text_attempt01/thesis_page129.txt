We note also that using more than2 samples can boost the overall performance of the
GAN reconstruction. For instance, using5 samples for the GAN reconstruction can boost
the overall performance on the method: paired with the GAN policy, it reaches up to
31.43 dB and 0.759 SSIM, and on LBCS, it goes up to32.10 dB and 0.773 PSNR. This
amounts to an improvement of0.8 to 1 dB and between0.03 to 0.04 SSIM. However,
averaging on increasingly many samples has a diminishing return, and in our experiments,
a plateau is reached around20 samples.
We also tried to perform sampling from the Gaussian NLL of Zhang et al. (2019b), and as
expected, the policy performed just like random sampling, as the NLL does not capture
the complexity of the posterior distribution.
