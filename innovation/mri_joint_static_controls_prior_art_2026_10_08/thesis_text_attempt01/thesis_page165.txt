dataset: LBCS outperforms both the Evaluator and the GAN, but it is interesting to
observe that, in this setting, the GAN performance is quite close to the one of LBCS,
especially in terms of SSIM. Note that in this context, the policy of Bakker et al. (2020)
tends to perform worse than previously.
