1.1 Related work
UAD methods for brain MRI. Reconstruction-based
methods train a generative model on healthy scans and
read anomalies from the residual between an input and its
pseudo-healthy reconstruction. Autoencoders and their
variants were surveyed by Baur et al. [9]. In our ear￾lier work, StRegA, we combined a compact context￾encoding VAE with pre- and post-processing steps cho￾sen for clinical data, and reported a Dice of 0.642 on
BraTS T2 tumours against 0.522 for the best base￾line [21]. Denoising diffusion probabilistic models [33] im￾proved reconstruction fidelity but tend either to copy
the anomaly or to hallucinate healthy tissue that differs
from the subject. Patched diffusion [10], automatic mask￾ing and re-sampling (AutoDDPM) [14], implicit guidance
(THOR) [15], masked diffusion (MAD-AD) [13], cold￾diffusion restorations (DISYRE) [51] and aggregated nor￾mative diffusion (ANDi) [29] each address this dilemma
differently. Kascenas et al. showed that the spatial scale
and magnitude of the training noise largely determine
how well denoising autoencoders and diffusion models de￾tect anomalies, and that a denoising autoencoder trained
with coarse noise is competitive with far more complex
models [37]. Two lines condition the denoiser on the in￾put itself: cDDPM conditions on a learnt encoding of the
image so that the reconstruction keeps the subject’s inten￾sity characteristics [11], and UCAD-CDM (here UCCD)
conditions on a contrastive encoder trained to be invari￾ant to the anomaly factor [54]. REFLECT replaces it￾erative sampling by a rectified flow [44] in the latent
space of a VAE, transporting an abnormal latent to
the healthy manifold in essentially one Euler step [12].
Feature-based methods compare deep features with those
of healthy references: AnomalyDINO uses frozen DI￾NOv2 [53] patch features and nearest-neighbour distances
to a memory bank [24]; Dinomaly and Dinomaly2 re￾construct foundation-model features [31,30]; SCRD4AD
uses scale-aware contrastive reverse distillation [41]. Iter￾Mask3D refines masks iteratively in 3D at test time [43].
We study REFLECT, UCCD, cDDPM and Anomaly￾DINO, which cover one-step transport, two forms of con￾ditioned diffusion and a training-free foundation-model
memory bank.
