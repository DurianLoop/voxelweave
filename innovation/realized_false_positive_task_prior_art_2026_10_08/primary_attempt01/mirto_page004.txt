2.3 Registration gating

has exactly one reference Ci
, the original whole-tumour
mask on the canonical grid, never resampled, cropped or
masked per method, and identical for every method. The
voxel volume is fixed from the dataset specification and
the array shape, not from file headers, which had been
altered in our copies of the data (Appendix B). Every
burden in this paper is therefore on the canonical grid.
