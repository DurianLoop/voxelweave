Lesions and their matching. Reference lesions are the 6-
connected components of Ci of at least 50 mm3
; predicted
components use the same connectivity. Components are
matched one to one by maximum-weight bipartite match￾ing on the IoU matrix [39], and a lesion counts as detected
if its matched IoU is at least 0.10. With H hits amongst
NL reference lesions the pooled sensitivity is H/NL. Be￾cause no sub-floor fragment was hit in our data, changing
the floor changes NL but not H, and the sensitivity scales
by the ratio of denominators (Section 3.7). We do not use
“matched Dice” computed on the reference region alone: if
P ⊆ C, then D = 2r/(1+r) with coverage r = |P|/|C|, so
it measures coverage only. Lesions are called small below
17,414.67 mm3
, the first tertile of above-floor validation
lesion volumes, computed on validation data only.
