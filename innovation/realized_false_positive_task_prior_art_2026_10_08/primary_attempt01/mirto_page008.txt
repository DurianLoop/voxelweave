2.7 The evaluation multiverse
Axes. The multiverse spans ten axes of evaluation choices
(Table 1): reference (canonical; the method’s own ref￾erence mapped to canonical space; the canonical ref￾erence after a round trip through the method’s grid),
support, voxel volume, threshold protocol, burden, post￾processing, lesion definition, hit criterion, aggregation and
metric. Their full factorial gives 3· 2·2· 5· 4· 3· 3·4·3·6 =
155,520 universes per method. The defensible subset fixes
the canonical reference and the true voxel volume and
admits only the validation, conformal and test-matched
threshold protocols: 15,552 universes, 2,592 per metric.
Axes that do not affect a metric replicate its value, which
keeps the design balanced and weights metrics equally.
