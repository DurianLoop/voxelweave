2.10 Data
Test and validation data. BraTS 2020 [49,4,5] pro￾vides multi-institutional pre-operative glioma MRI, co￾registered to a common template, resampled to 1 mm3
and skull-stripped by the organisers. We evaluate on T2;
FLAIR is used only by the FLAIR baseline. The refer￾ence is the whole tumour (labels 1, 2 and 4). We use 312
test subjects and 24 disjoint validation subjects, split at
random. The canonical arrays are our copy of the data,
cropped through-plane to remove empty slices: 240 × 240
in-plane at 1 mm and 98 to 149 slices (median 139); crop￾ping removes no tumour voxel. Median whole-tumour vol￾ume is 91.7 cm3
(IQR 53.2–147.8; range 7.3–361.8). The
test reference contains 5,473 6-connected components, of
which 472 reach the 50 mm3 floor (165 small, 307 large).
Healthy training data. IXI [35] provides T1 and T2 scans
from three London sites; our preprocessed T2 pool con￾tains the Hammersmith Hospital (HH, 3 T) and Guy’s
Hospital (Guys, 1.5 T) sites. MOOD [65] provides T1
brain scans. From these we formed five training sets
(Table 2), named after their sources, and tested every
model on BraTS T2. All main-text results use IXI￾HH+Guys for every method. The choice rests on
two grounds stated without reference to any score: IXI￾HH+Guys matches the test contrast, and IXI-HH and
IXI-Guys are strict subsets of it. Because all twenty
method–training-set combinations were scored on the
same test cohort, the choice is protected by its stated
rationale, by showing that each evaluation finding holds
with every set of training data (Appendix E), and
by a confirmatory evaluation on a new cohort, to be
pre-registered with this training set fixed. Within IXI￾HH+Guys each method kept its own training protocol
(REFLECT 504 volumes; UCCD 324 training and 81 val￾idation volumes; cDDPM 361 and 92; AnomalyDINO a
memory bank from 50 reference volumes), so a common
training set means the same source data, not the same
optimisation budget (Appendix E).

3.1 Registration and geometry
Hypothesis. A mapping error that permutes voxels within
slices should push per-subject voxel AUROC below 0.5
and scramble its ranks across subjects, whilst leaving
slice-level scores almost unchanged, because a slice that
contains tumour still does so after any in-plane permu￾tation. If so, label-free diagnostics can detect such er￾rors without extra labels. H7 (diagnostic power): rank
stability and the anti-location rate each detect in-plane
flips and transposes with power of at least 0.95 at a
false-alarm rate of 0.05 in 60 subjects. A secondary hy￾pothesis predicts that UCCD’s voxel AUROC falls un￾der antero-posterior and left-right flips whereas Anomaly￾DINO’s slice-level AUROC does not fall under an antero￾posterior flip.
A mapping error that standard metrics miss. Each
method stores its maps in its own axis order, crop and
resolution, so any comparison of methods with differ￾ent preprocessing must undo these steps, and an er￾ror in doing so is easy to make and hard to see.
