A scorer that knows only how much brain a
slice contains reached a marginal slice AUROC of
0.884 [0.869, 0.897], within 0.025 of every residual model
(0.887 to 0.907; Table 8, Fig. 10). Covariate adjust￾ment lowered the residual models to 0.73–0.77, but it
did not remove anatomy entirely: at the primary caliper
of 0.2 standard deviations the brain-area control still
scored 0.668 [0.654, 0.682], so H9 was not supported,
and 0.560 at 0.05. The spline-regression estimator also
failed its own control (0.626 with 5 degrees of freedom,
0.719 with 8). The obstacle is positivity: tumour slices
sit in large mid-brain slices, so within a subject there
are few negative slices of comparable area, and match￾ing kept only 59% of positives at 0.2 SD and 46% at
0.05 SD. The borrowed-map contrast avoids the prob￾lem by changing the scores rather than the compari￾son set: every model retained subject-specific informa￾tion, observed minus borrowed AROC being +0.229 (RE￾FLECT), +0.266 (UCCD), +0.271 (cDDPM) and +0.124
(AnomalyDINO), all intervals excluding zero, so the sec￾ondary hypothesis held, for the other two models as well.
