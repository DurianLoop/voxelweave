3.7 Lesion-level detection
Hypotheses. If no sub-floor fragment is ever hit, the lesion
floor changes pooled sensitivity only through its denom￾inator, by the ratio |Lf |/|L0| (Appendix A). H4 (con￾trast dependence): detection rises with T2 contrast and
with oedema fraction in every residual model. Secondary
hypotheses predict that UCCD finds fewer than 10% of
small lesions and that the three residual models miss
largely the same lesions (Jaccard index of the missed
sets of UCCD with REFLECT and with cDDPM at least
0.70).
The lesion definition rescaled pooled sensitivity about
elevenfold through the denominator alone. REFLECT hit
287 lesions under both definitions; 287/472 = 0.608 with
the 50 mm3 floor and 287/5,473 = 0.052 without, a ratio
of 5,473/472 = 11.6 that is identical for every method
because the 5,001 sub-floor fragments were never hit.
Subject-mean sensitivity moved less (0.765 against 0.173).
A sensitivity computed over all components is not a sen￾sitivity in any clinical sense, and a lesion-level metric is
uninterpretable without its connectivity and floor.
At the validation threshold the residual models found
nearly every large lesion and almost no small one: large￾lesion sensitivity was 0.915 to 0.997 and small-lesion sen￾sitivity 0.036 to 0.073, at most 12 of 165 small lesions,
as the secondary hypothesis predicted. The three resid￾ual models missed largely the same lesions (Jaccard in￾dex of the missed sets 0.805 to 0.938), which supports
the second secondary hypothesis. Most misses carried ev￾idence that the threshold did not select: of the 472 above￾floor lesions, 123 to 137 were missed with in-lesion scores
above the subject’s healthy-brain median, 9 to 17 had no
such evidence, and 12 to 35 were selected but delineated
with IoU < 0.10 (Fig. 11a). Lesion volume dominated de￾tection in every model (Firth-penalised log-odds 4.8 to
6.0 per standard deviation of log volume for the resid￾ual models; Fig. 11b), whereas in the Firth fit T2 con￾trast added an effect whose interval excluded zero only
for REFLECT (+1.98 [1.22, 2.82]) and AnomalyDINO
(+0.29 [0.03, 0.55]); for cDDPM only the GEE interval
excluded zero (Appendix Table 47). H4 was therefore not
supported; it could be tested only in part, because oedema
fractions are not available in the binary labels. Label￾informed upper bounds that respect the per-subject bud￾get (Appendix D.7, Table 45) place the remaining head￾room mainly in delineation: perfect boundaries for de￾tected lesions would add 0.40 to 0.46 Dice at 20 cm3
, an
optimal threshold per lesion 0.12 to 0.16, and an optimal
threshold per subject 0.03 to 0.07.
