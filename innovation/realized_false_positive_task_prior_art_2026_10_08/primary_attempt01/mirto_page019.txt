4 Discussion
The same four models produced different winners in four
distinct ways. The metric decides whether REFLECT or
a conditioned diffusion model leads. The aggregation de￾cides it again at a fixed budget, and Identity 2 explains
the direction. The operating point turns an advantage of
UCCD over cDDPM that is significant after Holm adjust￾ment into nothing once both are held to the same realised
burden, and Identity 1 accounts for the whole difference as
a difference in threshold transfer. And the training data,
changed in the same way for every method, reverses a
significant contrast. None of these is exotic. Each corre￾sponds to a decision that a typical UAD paper takes once,
