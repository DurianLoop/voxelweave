Limitations. The evidence comes from a single cohort
and a single contrast. BraTS 2020 whole-tumour labels
are large, conspicuous lesions, and evaluation on T2 alone
limits what can be said about small or subtle pathology;
the small-lesion results here are a floor, not a characteri￾sation. The 312 test subjects served both to develop the
protocol and the choice of training set and to test the
hypotheses, so all inference is exploratory; explicit deci￾sion criteria keep a hypothesis from being bent to fit its
outcome, but they cannot remove the influence of other
analyses of the same cohort. One trained model exists
per method and training set, so training-seed variance,
which can be large enough to change benchmark con￾clusions [17] and is the most likely competing explana￾tion for small between-method differences, is unknown.
A common training set means the same source data, not
the same optimisation budget, and IXI-HH+Guys is not
the best training set for every method: on test it cost RE￾FLECT 0.055 and UCCD 0.037 Dice relative to their best
training sets (Appendix Table 60), so the comparison of
methods should not be read as each method’s best attain￾able result. The injection study validated the diagnostics
on two pipelines; their power on other architectures is
assumed rather than shown. Validation-threshold inter￾vals do not include the uncertainty of fitting a threshold
on 24 subjects, which a nested resampling would add.
The covariate-adjusted slice estimators failed their own
control, so the borrowed-map contrast, not the adjusted
AUROC, is the defensible slice-level measure. Finally, the
training-free improvement to REFLECT is shown for one
method and two trained models, and the specific winning
aggregation did not replicate.
Future work. The next step is a pre-registered confirma￾tory evaluation on an untouched cohort, with the training
set and every threshold rule fixed by its protocol. Nested
resampling of the validation threshold, several training
seeds per method and a second pathology with smaller
lesions would close the main gaps listed above.
5 Conclusion
A UAD leaderboard is a statement about models and
evaluation jointly. In our study the method decided rank￾ings by voxel AUROC, whereas the evaluation decided the
order of the three strongest models in Dice and in lesion
sensitivity. MIRTO separates the two. It gates the geom￾etry of the comparison with tests of known power, holds
models to operating points chosen without the test set
and reports what those operating points actually deliver,
evaluates every conclusion over a multiverse of defensi￾ble pipelines, reading how often an order flips together
with how large each difference is, and attaches paired,
multiplicity-adjusted intervals to each claim. Elementary
identities explain why a contrast changes between valida￾tion thresholds and matched burden, why post-processing
appears to help and why pooled and subject-level rank￾ings disagree, and a known conformal result states how
often calibration on a small set will miss its target. Ap￾plied to four current UAD families, the protocol exposed
a geometric error that standard metrics missed, showed
which apparent differences were artefacts of threshold
transfer, and still supported some definite statements for
this cohort and training set: REFLECT had the highest
voxel AUROC, the two conditioned diffusion models gave
higher Dice at small budgets, no method found more than
12 of the 165 small lesions, and a training-free change to
REFLECT’s latent aggregation raised its Dice at matched
burden in two checkpoints. Reporting evaluation choices
with the same care as architectures would make UAD
comparisons much easier to interpret.
