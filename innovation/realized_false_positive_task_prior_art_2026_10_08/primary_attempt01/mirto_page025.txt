The Jaccard bound and the triangle inequality. The Jaccard distance is a metric on finite sets (it is the Steinhaus
transform of the symmetric-difference metric). For any P, dJ (P, RA) ≤ dJ (P, RB) + dJ (RB, RA) and symmetrically,
which gives Eq. (9).
Coverage-only “matched Dice”. If P ⊆ C then TP = |P| and D = 2|P|/(|P| + |C|) = 2r/(1 + r) with r = |P|/|C|, a
strictly increasing function of coverage alone that is blind to false positives.
Lesion-definition invariance of the hit count. Let L0 be all reference components and Lf ⊆ L0 those above the floor.
If no component of L0 \ Lf is hit, the pooled sensitivities satisfy S0/Sf = |Lf |/|L0| for every method, which is what
we observe (472/5,473).
