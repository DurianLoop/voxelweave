B Physical units and voxel volumes
The method pipelines’ own files declared voxel volumes of 0.8786 mm3
(one shared grid), 1.166 mm3
(UCCD) and
0.604 mm3
(cDDPM) for grids whose true voxel was 1 mm3 or 0.8789 mm3
(256 × 256 in-plane). The same masks
therefore produced burdens that differed by a factor of 1.93 between UCCD and cDDPM, and a nominal 20 cm3
meant 17.2 cm3
for one and 33.1 cm3
for the other. MIRTO recovers the true grid from voxel counts alone: the ratio
of the tumour volume in those files, divided by the declared voxel volume, to the canonical voxel count is 1.0004 for
the 240-pixel grid and 1.1380 ≈ (256/240)2 = 1.1378 for the 256-pixel grid. A round trip of the canonical reference
through the UCCD/cDDPM native grid (2 mm in-plane, through-plane decimation) loses 57% of the 6-connected
components, mostly sub-floor fragments, and 1.8% of tumour volume, whilst the REFLECT and AnomalyDINO
grids lose 0.01–0.02% (Table 21). No MIRTO metric is computed on a method’s own resampled reference. Table 10
describes the cohort, and Tables 11 and 12 give the voxel-count check and its consequence for the budget.
