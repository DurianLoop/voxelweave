Table 16 gives the bias of every headline metric under every injected corruption for both verified pipelines, and
Table 17 gives the power of the rank-stability and anti-location diagnostics. Rank stability detects every geometric
corruption at n = 30, including through-plane shifts that barely change the metrics, which is why we read it as a
change detector. The anti-location rate detects the in-plane errors at n = 30 and otherwise reaches a power of 0.95
only at n = 312, for two AnomalyDINO conditions (50% identifier permutation and doubled reference volume). A 5%
permutation of subject identifiers is not detectable by either diagnostic at n ≤ 312, and a 20% permutation only by
rank stability at n = 312. Removing sub-floor reference fragments changed no metric, as expected, since no method
ever hit one.
