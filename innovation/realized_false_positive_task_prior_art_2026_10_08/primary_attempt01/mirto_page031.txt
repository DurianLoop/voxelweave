D.3 Operating points and risk control
Table 29 gives the threshold-transfer gap with the thresholds involved and with the median of the per-subject
differences, which agrees with the mean in sign at every budget and method except UCCD at 40 cm3
, where both
are indistinguishable from zero. Table 30 lists every threshold rule at 20 cm3
, and Table 31 the exceedance and Dice
of each rule at every budget. Conformal risk control (CRC) at α = 0.05 and RCPS at α = 0.10 select the same, very
strict, thresholds and keep exceedance at or below 0.019, at a large Dice cost. The label-free CRC, which bounds
the predicted volume rather than the false-positive volume, is the most conservative rule for UCCD and cDDPM.
Table 32 gives the random-split results per method that Table 5 summarises. The 0.005 threshold grid limits burden
matching to a relative mismatch of at most 5.7%.
