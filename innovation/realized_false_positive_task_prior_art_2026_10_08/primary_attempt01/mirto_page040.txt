Table 56 summarises the comparison with a common training set. Within IXI-HH+Guys the methods kept their
own training protocols: REFLECT trained on all 504 IXI T2 volumes of the pool (stage 1, 200 epochs; re-flow,
50 epochs); UCCD on 324 training and 81 validation volumes (encoder and diffusion model taken at their best
validation loss); cDDPM on 361 training and 92 validation volumes (best validation loss at epoch 1,426 of 1,600,
2,854 optimisation steps); AnomalyDINO built its memory bank from 50 reference volumes with slice stride 2. The
optimisation budget of a single method varies strongly between training sets (cDDPM: 2,854 steps on IXI-HH+Guys,
79,040 on MOOD+IXI), so effects of the training set are effects of the training data together with the protocol that
came with it. One model was trained per method and training set, so variance between training runs is not estimated;
it is the most plausible competing explanation for the smaller differences between methods. The ranking-instability
and evaluation-dominance hypotheses were supported with every common training set (ϕmax from 0.295 to 0.485).
