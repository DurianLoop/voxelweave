F Secondary hypotheses and deviations
Table 9 gives the nine primary hypotheses. Table 63 gives the eight secondary hypotheses, each a prediction about
a specific effect that follows from the identities of Section 2 or from the primary analyses, tested with all methods
trained on IXI-HH+Guys. Deviations from the stated analyses are listed after the table.
Deviations and disclosures. (1) The stated description of the training data names all three IXI sites; our IXI T2
data contain no subjects from the third site (IOP), so IXI-HH+Guys comprises the Hammersmith and Guy’s sites,
identically for all methods. (2) The canonical arrays are a through-plane-cropped copy (98–149 slices) of the 240 ×
240×155 originals; no tumour voxel is affected. (3) The contrast-dependence hypothesis could be tested only in part,
because the available labels are binary. (4) The pruning trade-off was judged from point estimates, as no interval was
computed for it. (5) The stratified and matched slice-level estimators named in H9 fail their covariate-only check; the
regression estimator, added as an exploratory alternative, fails as well. (6) The family of six latent-aggregation rules
specified for this experiment could not be computed from the stored latents; the family reported here is exploratory.
(7) A pre-specified through-plane offset check was positive for AnomalyDINO, whose voxel AUROC peaks three slices
from the canonical position (+0.008); its export was verified to be identical to the canonical data and its numbers
