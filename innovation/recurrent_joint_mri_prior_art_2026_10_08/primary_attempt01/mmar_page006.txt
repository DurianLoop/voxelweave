Clinical reality dictates that datasets frequently contain missing modali￾ties. To expand the pool of usable training data for WMH and ISL segmen￾tation, our goal is to leverage all available patient data to improve feature
representation rather than discarding subjects with incomplete scans. To
systematically evaluate this hetero-modal learning setting, we derived three
different training data distributions from our dataset, illustrated in Figure
