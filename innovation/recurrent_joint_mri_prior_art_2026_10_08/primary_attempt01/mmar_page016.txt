3.6.2. Test time corruption
Because our dataset lacks truly corrupted images, we simulated catas￾trophic degradation during testing using Gaussian noise (std=10) and bias
field artefacts (polynomial degree 2, coefficients in [0.7, 0.9]). These parame￾ters differ substantially from the training augmentations to reduce evaluation
bias. We also introduced a “false present” scenario—setting an entire scan
to zero except for a single corner voxel—simulating a missing scan that by￾passes automated missing-data detection. Figure 5 shows an example of

4.1. Modality specialists win when trained on fully multi-modal data
