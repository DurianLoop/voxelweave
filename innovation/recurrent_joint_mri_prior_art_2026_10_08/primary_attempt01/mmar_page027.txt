Figure 6: Visualisation of MMAR attention weights. Visualised via maximum
intensity projection over the channels of the MMAR attention weights at the highest
resolution level of the encoder when a FLAIR scan from the validation set is subjected to
different missingness and corruption scenarios. The figure shows that global corruption
(false present and Gaussian noise) results in similar attention maps to truly missing, and
that local corruption (bias field) leads to only locally reduced attention.
