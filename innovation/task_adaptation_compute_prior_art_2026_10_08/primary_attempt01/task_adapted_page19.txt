7.3.1. Joint tomographic reconstruction and classiﬁcation.
T ask: Recover probabilities that a 2D grey scale MNIST image is a 0,1, . . . ,9 from noisy
parallel beam tomographic data (see section 6.2.1).
Data: Elements in Y are real-valued functions representing samples of a Poisson random
variable with mean equal to the exponential of the parallel beam ray transform and an
intensity corresponding to 60 photons/line. The ray transform is digitized by sampling
the angular variable at 5 uniformly sampled points in [0 ,π ] with 25 lines/angle.

ﬁxed rectangular region Ω⊂ R2, so X :=L2(Ω, R). These are discretized by sampling
on a uniform 28× 28 grid. The loss 𝓁X : X×X→ R is the squared L2-distance on X.
