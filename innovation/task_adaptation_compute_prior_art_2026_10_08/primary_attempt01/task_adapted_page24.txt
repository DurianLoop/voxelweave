The segmentation is a normalized grey-scale image denoting the probability that a point
belongs to the segmented structure. The choice C = 0.9 seems to be a good compromise
for a good reconstruction and segmentation (see Figure 3). Note also that C→ 1 gives the
sequential approach, so C = 0.999 may serve as a proxy for it. Reconstructions take a few
milliseconds to perform on a desktop gaming PC.
