with 30 or 70 iterations required in IRCNN and BM3D-MRI
respectively.
4.3. Phase retrieval
The goal of phase retrieval (PR) is to recover the underlying
image from only the amplitude, or intensity of the output of
a complex linear system. Mathematically, PR can be deﬁned
as the problem of recovering a signalx∈ RN or CN from
measurement y of the form y2 =|Ax|2 +ω, where the
measurement matrixA represents the forward operator of
the system, andω represents shot noise. We approximate it
withω∼N (0,α 2|Ax|2). The term α controls the sigma-
to-noise ratio in this problem.

random spatial modulator (SLM) patterns are recorded. We
model the capture of four measurements using a phase-only
SLM as (Metzler et al., 2018). Each measurement opera-
tor can be mathematically described as Ai =FDi, i ∈
