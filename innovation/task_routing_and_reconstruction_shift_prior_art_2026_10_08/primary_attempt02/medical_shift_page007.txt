4.2. Impact of Reconstruction on Fairness
Although aggregate task performance is largely unaffected, reconstruction could still alter
relative performance across demographic subgroups. To test this possibility, we evaluated
fairness on the downstream models using acceleration factor 8 for UCSF-PDGM and a
photon count of 10,000 for CheXpert, representing the middle noise levels.
