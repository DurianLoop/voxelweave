Reconstruction Hyperparameters.No data augmentation was applied to any of the
reconstruction pipelines. A U-Net was trained for 20 epochs on both UCSF-PDGM and
CheXpert, using Adam with MSE loss, a learning rate of 0.001, and a batch size of 16. The
GAN (Pix2Pix) was trained for 200 epochs on each dataset with Adam, a learning rate
0.0002, and a batch size of 32 to compensate for the smaller data volume. For the SDE
